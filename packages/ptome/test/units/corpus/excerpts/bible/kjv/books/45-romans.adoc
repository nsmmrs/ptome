== @ROM

[.book-title]
The Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Romans

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul Greets the Saints in Rome

@ Paul, ##a servant##note:x[<<Rom 15:16>>] of Jesus Christ, ##called##note:x[<<Acts 9:15>>] __to be__ an apostle, separated unto ##the gospel##note:x[<<Rom 15:16>>] of God,
@ ( ##Which##note:x[<<Acts 10:43; Titus 1:2>>] he had promised afore ##by##note:x[<<Rom 3:21>>] his prophets in the holy scriptures,)
@ Concerning ##his Son##note:x[<<Gal 4:4>>] Jesus Christ our Lord, ##which##note:x[<<Matt 1:1>>] was made of the seed of David ##according##note:x[<<Gal 4:4>>] to the flesh;
@ And declared __to be__ ##the Son##note:x[<<Eph 1:19–23; Heb 5:5–6>>] of God with power, ##according##note:x[<<Heb 9:14>>] to the spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead:
@ By whom we have received grace and apostleship, ##for obedience to the faith##note:x[<<Acts 6:7; Rom 16:26>>] among all nations, ##for his name##note:x[<<Mal 1:11>>]:
@ Among whom are ye also ##the called##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:9; 2 Pet 1:10; Rev 17:14>>] of Jesus Christ:
@ ##To all##note:x[<<Col 1:2; Jude 1:1>>] that be in Rome, beloved of God, called __to be__ saints: ##Grace##note:x[<<Col 1:2>>] to you and peace from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Unashamed of the Gospel

@ First, ##I thank##note:x[<<Rom 6:17>>] my God through Jesus Christ for you all, ##that your##note:x[<<Rom 16:19; 1 Thess 1:8–9>>] faith is spoken of throughout the whole world.
@ For ##God##note:x[<<Rom 9:1>>] is my witness, ##whom##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:3>>] I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, ##that##note:x[<<Eph 6:18>>] without ceasing I make mention of you always in my prayers;
@ Making ##request##note:x[<<Rom 15:30–32; Phlm 1:22>>], if by any means now at length I might have a prosperous journey ##by the will##note:x[<<Acts 18:21>>] of God to come unto you.
@ For I long to see you, that I may impart unto you some spiritual gift, ##to the##note:x[<<Rom 16:25; 2 Thess 3:3; 2 Pet 1:12>>] end ye may be established;
@ That is, ##that I may##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:7–10>>] be comforted together with you by the mutual faith both of you and me.
@ Now I would not have you ignorant, brethren, ##that oftentimes##note:x[<<Acts 19:21>>] I purposed to come unto you, ( but was let hitherto,) ##that I##note:x[<<Phil 4:17>>] might have some fruit among you also, ##even##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:17>>] as among other Gentiles.
@ I am ##debtor##note:x[<<Acts 9:15; 1 Cor 9:16–23>>] ##both to##note:x[<<1 Cor 9:16>>] the Greeks, and to the Barbarians; both to the wise, and to the unwise.
@ So, as much as in me is, ##I am##note:x[<<Isa 6:8; 1 Cor 9:17; 2 Cor 10:15–16>>] ready to preach the gospel to you that are at Rome also.
@ For ##I am##note:x[<<Mark 8:38; 2 Tim 1:8; 1 Pet 4:16>>] not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.
@ For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, ##The just##note:x[<<Hab 2:4; Gal 3:11; Heb 10:38>>] shall live by faith.

[discrete.pericope]
==== God’s Wrath against Sin

@ For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, ##who hold##note:x[<<Rom 1:32; 2 Thess 2:10; 1 Tim 4:1–2>>] the truth in unrighteousness;
@ Because ##that which##note:x[<<Ps 19:1–6; Acts 14:16; Rom 1:20>>] may be known of God is manifest in them; for God hath shewed __it__ unto them.
@ For the invisible things of him ##from the##note:x[<<Ps 8:3; Rom 1:19>>] creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, ##__even__ his##note:x[<<Isa 40:26>>] eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:
@ Because that, when they knew God, they glorified __him__ not as God, neither were thankful; ##but became##note:x[<<2 Kgs 17:15; Jer 2:5; Eph 4:17–18>>] vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened.
@ ##Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools,##note:x[<<Prov 26:12; Rom 11:25; 1 Cor 1:19–21>>]
@ And ##changed##note:x[<<Ps 106:20; Jer 2:11>>] the glory of the uncorruptible God into ##an image##note:x[<<Acts 17:29>>] made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.

@ Wherefore ##God##note:x[<<Ps 81:11–12; Matt 15:14; Acts 7:42>>] also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves:
@ Who changed the truth of God ##into a lie##note:x[<<Jer 13:25; Hab 2:18>>], and worshipped and served ##the creature##note:x[<<1 John 2:15–16>>] more than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.
@ For this cause God gave them up unto ##vile##note:x[<<Lev 18:22–28; 1 Tim 1:10; Jude 1:7>>] affections: for even their women did change the natural use into that which is against nature:
@ And likewise also the men, leaving the natural use of the woman, burned in their lust one toward another; men with men working that which is unseemly, and receiving in themselves ##that recompence##note:x[<<Rom 1:23–24>>] of their error which was meet.
@ And even ##as they did##note:x[<<Rom 1:21; 2 Thess 2:10–12>>] not like to ##retain##note:x[<<Titus 1:16>>] God in __their__ knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind, to do those things which are not convenient;
@ Being filled with all unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness; full of envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity; ##whisperers##note:x[<<Ps 41:7; Prov 16:28; 2 Cor 12:20>>],
@ Backbiters, haters of God, despiteful, proud, ##boasters##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:18; Jude 1:16>>], inventors of evil things, ##disobedient##note:x[<<2 Tim 3:2>>] to parents,
@ note:x[<<2 Tim 3:3>>]##Without understanding##note:x[<<Prov 18:2; Rom 3:11>>], covenantbreakers, without natural affection, implacable, unmerciful:
@ Who ##knowing##note:x[<<Rom 1:21; 2:1–5>>] the judgment of God, that they which commit such things are ##worthy##note:x[<<Rom 6:21>>] of death, not only do the same, but have pleasure in them that do them.

[discrete.pericope]
==== God Remains Faithful

@ Much every way: chiefly, ##because##note:x[<<Neh 9:13–14; Rom 9:4>>] that unto them were committed ##the oracles##note:x[<<Acts 7:38>>] of God.
@ For what ##if some##note:x[<<Rom 9:6; Heb 4:2>>] did not believe? ##shall##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:13>>] their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?
@ ##God forbid##note:x[<<Gal 2:17>>]: yea, let God be true, ##but every##note:x[<<Ps 116:11>>] man a liar; as it is written, ##That thou##note:x[<<Ps 51:4>>] mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.
@ But if our unrighteousness commend the righteousness of God, what shall we say? ##__Is__ God##note:x[<<Rom 2:5>>] unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (##I speak##note:x[<<Rom 6:19; Gal 3:15>>] as a man)

@ And not __rather__, ( as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) ##Let us##note:x[<<Rom 6:1, 15>>] do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just.

[.parallel]

@ And ##I##note:x[<<2 Pet 1:12; 1 John 2:21>>] myself also am persuaded of you, my brethren, that ye also are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, ##able##note:x[<<Col 3:16>>] also to admonish one another.

@ I have therefore ##whereof##note:x[<<2 Cor 2:14–16; 7:4; 12:11–21>>] I may glory through Jesus Christ in those things which pertain to God.
