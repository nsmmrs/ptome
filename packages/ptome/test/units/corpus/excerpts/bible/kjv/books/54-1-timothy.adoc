== @1TI

[.book-title]
The First Epistle of Paul the Apostle to Timothy

=== @6

[discrete.pericope]
==== Serving with Honor

[.parallel]
(<<Ephesians 6:5–9; Colossians 3:22–25>>)

@ Let as many servants as are under the yoke ##count##note:x[<<Eph 6:5–8; 1 Pet 2:17–20>>] their own masters worthy of all honour, ##that the##note:x[<<Titus 2:5>>] name of God and __his__ doctrine be not blasphemed.
@ And they that have ##believing##note:x[<<Col 4:1>>] masters, let them not despise __them__, because they are brethren; but rather do __them__ service, because they are faithful and beloved, partakers of the benefit. ##These##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:11>>] things teach and exhort.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Reject False Doctrines

@ If ##any##note:x[<<Rom 16:17; 1 Tim 1:3>>] man teach otherwise, and consent not ##to wholesome##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:10>>] words, __even__ the words of our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the doctrine which is according to godliness;
@ ##He##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:6; 2 Tim 3:4>>] is proud, knowing nothing, but doting ##about##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:23>>] questions and strifes of words, where of cometh envy, strife, railings, evil surmisings,
@ Perverse disputings of men of corrupt minds, and destitute of the truth, ##supposing##note:x[<<Titus 1:11; 2 Pet 2:3>>] that gain is godliness: ##from##note:x[<<2 Tim 3:5>>] such withdraw thyself.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Godliness with Contentment

@ But ##godliness##note:x[<<Heb 13:5>>] with ##contentment##note:x[<<Phil 4:11–13; 1 Tim 6:8>>] is great gain.
@ For ##we brought##note:x[<<Job 1:21>>] nothing into __this__ world, __and it is__ ##certain##note:x[<<Ps 49:17; Luke 12:20–21>>] we can carry no thing out.
@ ##And having food and raiment let us be therewith content.##note:x[<<Matt 6:11, 25–33; Heb 13:5–6>>]
@ But ##they##note:x[<<Prov 15:27; 28:20–22>>] that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and __into__ ##many##note:x[<<1 John 2:15–17>>] foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition.
@ For ##the love##note:x[<<Prov 1:19>>] of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, ##and pierced##note:x[<<Ps 32:10; Prov 1:31>>] themselves through with many sorrows.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Fight the Good Fight

@ ##But##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:22>>] thou, O man of God, ##flee##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:22>>] these things; ##and##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:22>>] follow after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness.
@ note:x[<<1 Pet 5:10>>]##Fight##note:x[<<1 Cor 9:25–26; 1 Tim 1:18>>] the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, where unto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.
@ I ##give##note:x[<<1 Tim 5:21>>] thee charge in the sight of God, who quickeneth all things, and __before__ Christ Jesus, ##who before##note:x[<<Matt 27:11; John 18:36–37>>] Pontius Pilate witnessed a good confession;
@ That thou keep __this__ commandment ##without##note:x[<<2 Pet 3:14>>] spot, unrebukeable, ##until##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:23; 1 Pet 1:7>>] the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ:
@ Which in his times he shall shew, ##__who__##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:17>>] __is__ the blessed and only Potentate, ##the King##note:x[<<Rev 17:14; 19:16>>] of kings, and Lord of lords;
@ Who ##only##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:17>>] hath immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto; ##whom##note:x[<<John 1:18>>] no man hath seen, nor can see: ##to whom##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:17>>] __be__ honour and power everlasting. Amen.

[discrete.pericope]
==== A Charge to the Rich

[.parallel]
(<<Proverbs 23:1–5; James 5:1–6>>)

@ Charge them that are ##rich##note:x[<<Matt 19:23>>] in this world, ##that they##note:x[<<Jas 1:9–10>>] be not highminded, nor ##trust##note:x[<<Prov 11:28>>] in uncertain riches, but in the living God, who giveth us richly all things to enjoy;
@ That ##they do##note:x[<<Heb 13:16>>] good, that they be rich in good works, ##ready##note:x[<<Heb 13:16; 1 John 3:17>>] to distribute, willing to communicate;
@ ##Laying##note:x[<<Matt 6:19–21>>] up in store for themselves a good ##foundation##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:19>>] against the time to come, that they may ##lay##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:12>>] hold on eternal life.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Guard the Faith

@ O Timothy, ##keep##note:x[<<Rom 3:2; 2 Tim 3:14>>] that which is committed to thy trust, avoiding profane __and__ vain babblings, and ##oppositions##note:x[<<Col 2:8>>] of science falsely so called:
@ Which some professing ##have##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:18>>] erred concerning the faith. Grace __be__ with thee. Amen.

[.colophon]
The first to Timothy was written from Laodicea, which is the chiefest city of Phrygia Pacatiana.
