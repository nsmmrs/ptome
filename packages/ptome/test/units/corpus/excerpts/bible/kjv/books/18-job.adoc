==== Zophar Rebukes Job

@ Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said,
@ Should not ##the multitude##note:x[<<Job 18:2; Prov 10:19; Jas 1:19>>] of words be answered? and should a man full of talk be justified?note:tn[__full…__: Heb. of lips]
@ Should thy ##lies##note:tn[or, devices] make men hold their peace? and when thou ##mockest##note:x[<<Job 17:2>>], shall no man make thee ashamed?
@ ##For thou##note:x[<<Job 6:10; 10:7>>] hast said, My doctrine __is__ pure, and I am clean in thine eyes.
@ But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee;
@ And that he would ##shew thee##note:x[<<Dan 2:28>>] the secrets of wisdom, that __they are__ double to that which is! Know therefore that ##God exacteth##note:x[<<Ezra 9:13; Lam 3:22>>] of thee __less__ than thine iniquity __deserveth__.

@ ##Canst##note:x[<<Ps 145:3; Eccl 3:11; Rom 11:33>>] thou by searching find out God? canst thou find out the Almighty unto perfection?
@ ##__It is__ as high as heaven##note:x[<<Job 22:12; Isa 55:9>>]; what canst thou do? ##deeper##note:x[<<Ps 139:6–8>>] than hell; what canst thou know?note:tn[__as high…__: Heb. the heights of heaven]
@ The measure thereof __is__ longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.
@ ##If he cut off##note:x[<<Job 9:12–13; 12:14>>], and ##shut up##note:x[<<Rev 3:7>>], or gather together, then who can hinder him?note:tn[__cut…__: or, make a change]note:tn[__hinder…__: Heb. turn him away?]
@ For ##he knoweth##note:x[<<Heb 4:13>>] vain men: ##he seeth##note:x[<<Ps 10:14; Heb 4:13>>] wickedness also; will he not then consider __it__?
@ note:x[<<Jas 2:20>>]For ##vain##note:tn[Heb. empty] man would be wise, though man be born __like__ ##a wild##note:x[<<Job 39:5–8>>] ass’s colt.

@ If thou ##prepare##note:x[<<1 Sam 7:3>>] thine heart, and ##stretch##note:x[<<Ps 88:9; 143:6>>] out thine hands toward him;
@ If iniquity __be__ in thine hand, ##put it far##note:x[<<Job 22:23>>] away, and let not wickedness dwell in thy tabernacles.
@ For then shalt thou ##lift up##note:x[<<Job 22:26; 1 John 2:28; 3:19–22>>] thy face without spot; yea, thou shalt be stedfast, and shalt not fear:
@ ##Because##note:x[<<Eccl 5:20; Isa 54:4; 65:16>>] thou shalt forget __thy__ misery, __and__ remember __it__ as waters __that__ pass away:
@ And __thine__ ##age##note:x[<<Ps 37:6; Prov 4:18; Isa 58:8–10>>] shall be clearer than the noonday; thou shalt shine forth, thou shalt be as the morning.note:tn[__be clearer…__: Heb. arise above]
@ And thou shalt be secure, ##because##note:x[<<Ps 43:5>>] there is hope; yea, thou shalt dig __about thee, and__ ##thou shalt take##note:x[<<Ps 3:5; Prov 3:24–26>>] thy rest in safety.
@ Also thou shalt lie down, and none shall make __thee__ afraid; yea, ##many##note:x[<<Job 42:8–9; Ps 45:12; Isa 60:14>>] shall make suit unto thee.note:tn[__make suit…__: Heb. intreat thy face]
@ But ##the eyes##note:x[<<Deut 28:65>>] of the wicked shall fail, and they shall not escape, and their hope __shall be as__ the giving up of the ghost.note:tn[__they shall…__: Heb. flight shall perish from them]note:tn[__the giving…__: or, a puff of breath]
