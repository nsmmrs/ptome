The Second Epistle of Paul the Apostle to Timothy

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Greeting to Timothy

[.parallel]
(<<1 Timothy 1:1–2>>)

@ Paul, ##an##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:1>>] apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, according to ##the promise##note:x[<<John 17:3; Heb 9:15>>] of life which is in Christ Jesus,
@ To ##Timothy##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:2>>], __my__ dearly beloved son: Grace, mercy, __and__ peace, from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Faithfulness under Persecution

[.parallel]
(<<Matthew 10:16–25>>)

@ ##I thank##note:x[<<Rom 1:8>>] God, ##whom##note:x[<<Acts 24:14>>] I serve from __my__ forefathers ##with##note:x[<<Heb 13:8>>] pure conscience, that without ceasing I have remembrance of thee in my prayers night and day;
@ Greatly ##desiring##note:x[<<Phil 1:8; 2 Tim 4:9, 21>>] to see thee, being mindful of thy tears, that I may be filled with joy;
@ When I call to remembrance the ##unfeigned##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:5; 4:6>>] faith that is in thee, which dwelt first in thy grandmother Lois, and ##thy mother##note:x[<<Acts 16:1>>] Eunice; and I am persuaded that in thee also.
@ Wherefore I put thee in remembrance ##that##note:x[<<Rom 12:6–8; 1 Pet 4:10–11>>] thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee ##by the##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:14>>] putting on of my hands.
@ For God hath not given us ##the spirit##note:x[<<Rom 8:15; 1 John 4:18>>] of fear; but of power, and ##of love##note:x[<<Gal 5:22>>], and of a sound mind.
@ Be not thou therefore ##ashamed##note:x[<<Mark 8:38; Rom 1:16>>] of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but ##be thou##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:5>>] partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God;
@ Who ##hath##note:x[<<Eph 2:8; Titus 3:4–5>>] saved us, and called __us__ with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, ##which##note:x[<<Eph 1:3>>] was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began,
@ But is ##now##note:x[<<Rom 16:26; 1 John 1:2>>] made manifest by the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who hath abolished death, ##and hath##note:x[<<1 John 1:2>>] brought life and immortality to light through the gospel:
@ note:x[<<Acts 9:15; Eph 3:7–8; 1 Tim 2:7>>]Where unto I am appointed a preacher, and an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.
@ For ##the which##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:8>>] cause I also suffer these things: nevertheless ##I am##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:8>>] not ashamed: for I know whom I have believed, and am persuaded that he is able to ##keep##note:x[<<Jude 1:24>>] that which I have committed unto him against that day.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Holding to Sound Teaching

@ ##Hold##note:x[<<2 Tim 3:14; Titus 1:9>>] fast the form of sound words, ##which##note:x[<<Phil 4:9>>] thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.
@ That ##good##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:20>>] thing which was committed unto thee keep ##by the##note:x[<<Rom 8:13>>] Holy Ghost ##which dwelleth##note:x[<<John 14:17>>] in us.
@ This thou knowest, that all they which are in Asia ##be##note:x[<<Phil 2:21; 2 Tim 4:16>>] turned away from me; of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.
@ The ##Lord##note:x[<<Heb 10:34>>] give mercy unto ##the house##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:19>>] of Onesiphorus; for he oft ##refreshed##note:x[<<Phlm 1:20>>] me, and was not ashamed of my chain:
@ But, when he was in Rome, he sought me out very diligently, and found __me__.
@ The Lord grant unto him that he may find mercy of the Lord ##in that##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:12>>] day: and in how many things he ##ministered##note:x[<<Heb 6:10>>] unto me at Ephesus, thou knowest very well.

=== @3

[discrete.pericope]
==== Evil in the Last Days

@ This know also, that ##in##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:1; 2 Tim 4:3; 2 Pet 3:3>>] the last days perilous times shall come.
@ For men shall be ##lovers##note:x[<<Phil 2:21>>] of their own selves, ##covetous##note:x[<<Rom 1:29>>], ##boasters##note:x[<<Rom 1:29–31>>], proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,
@ note:x[<<Jude 1:18>>]Without ##natural##note:x[<<Rom 1:31>>] affection, ##trucebreakers##note:x[<<Rom 1:31>>], false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good,
@ ##Traitors##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:10–22>>], heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than ##lovers of God##note:x[<<Phil 3:18–19; Jude 1:4>>];
@ Having ##a form##note:x[<<Isa 29:13; Matt 23:27–28; Titus 1:16>>] of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away.
@ For ##of this##note:x[<<Jude 1:4>>] sort are they which creep into houses, and lead captive silly women laden with sins, ##led##note:x[<<2 Pet 3:17>>] away with ##divers##note:x[<<Titus 3:3>>] lusts,
@ Ever ##learning##note:x[<<Eph 4:14; 2 Tim 4:3–4>>], and ##never##note:x[<<1 Tim 2:4>>] able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
@ Now ##as##note:x[<<Exod 7:11>>] Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so do these also ##resist##note:x[<<Acts 13:8–11; Rev 2:20>>] the truth: men of corrupt minds, reprobate concerning the faith.
@ But they shall proceed no further: for ##their##note:x[<<Exod 7:12; Jer 28:15–17; 2 Tim 3:8>>] folly shall be manifest unto all __men__, as theirs also was.

[discrete.pericope]
==== All Scripture Is God-Breathed

[.parallel]
(<<Hebrews 4:12–16>>)

@ But thou hast fully known ##my##note:x[<<Titus 2:7>>] doctrine, manner of life, purpose, ##faith##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:11; 2 Pet 1:5–7>>], longsuffering, charity, patience,
@ ##Persecutions##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:23–28>>], afflictions, which came unto me ##at Antioch##note:x[<<Acts 14:5–6>>], at Iconium, at Lystra; what persecutions I endured: ##but##note:x[<<Ps 34:19>>] out of __them__ all the Lord delivered me.
@ Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus ##shall##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:20–21; 3:14; 4:12–16>>] suffer persecution.
@ But ##evil##note:x[<<2 Thess 2:6–10; 1 Tim 4:1; 2 Tim 3:8>>] men and seducers shall wax worse and worse, deceiving, and being deceived.
@ But ##continue##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:13; 2:2>>] thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, ##knowing##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:13>>] of whom thou hast learned __them__;
@ And that ##from##note:x[<<Prov 22:6>>] a child thou hast known ##the holy##note:x[<<Luke 24:45; 2 Pet 1:20–21>>] scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.
@ ##All##note:x[<<Rom 15:4; Heb 4:12; 2 Pet 1:19–21>>] scripture __is__ given by inspiration of God, and __is__ profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:
@ That the man of God may be perfect, ##throughly furnished##note:x[<<2 Cor 9:8; Eph 2:10; 2 Tim 2:21>>] unto all good works.
