[discrete.pericope]
==== Jesus Our Advocate

@ My little children, these things write I unto you, that ye sin not. And if any man sin, ##we have##note:x[<<Rom 8:34; 1 Tim 2:5>>] an advocate with the ##Father##note:x[<<John 14:6>>], Jesus Christ the righteous:
@ And ##he is##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:18; 1 John 4:10>>] the propitiation for our sins: and not for ours only, but also ##for __the__##note:x[<<1 John 4:14>>] __sins of__ the whole world.
@ And hereby we do know that we know him, ##if we##note:x[<<John 14:15; 15:10; 1 John 5:3>>] keep his commandments.
@ He ##that saith##note:x[<<1 John 1:6>>], ##I know##note:x[<<Titus 1:16>>] him, and keepeth not his commandments, ##is a##note:x[<<1 John 1:6>>] liar, and the truth is not in him.
@ But ##whoso##note:x[<<John 14:21, 23>>] keepeth his word, ##in him##note:x[<<1 John 4:12>>] verily is the love of God perfected: hereby know we that we are in him.
@ He that saith he abideth in him ought himself also so ##to walk##note:x[<<John 13:15; 1 Cor 11:1; 1 Pet 2:21>>], even as he walked.

==== A New Commandment

@ Brethren, ##I write##note:x[<<1 John 3:11>>] no new commandment unto you, ##but##note:x[<<Lev 19:18; Mark 12:29–34>>] an old commandment which ye had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word which ye have heard from the beginning.
@ Again, ##a new##note:x[<<John 13:34>>] commandment I write unto you, which thing is true in him and in you: because ##the darkness##note:x[<<Rom 13:12; Eph 5:8>>] is past, and the true light now shineth.
@ He that saith he ##is in##note:x[<<Ps 82:5; 1 John 2:11>>] the light, ##and hateth##note:x[<<1 John 3:13–17>>] his brother, is in darkness even until now.
@ He ##that loveth##note:x[<<Hos 6:3; 1 John 3:14>>] his brother abideth in the light, and there is none ##occasion of stumbling##note:x[<<Rom 9:32–33>>] in him.
@ But ##he that##note:x[<<John 12:35; Titus 3:3>>] hateth his brother is in darkness, ##and walketh##note:x[<<John 12:35>>] in darkness, and knoweth not whither he goeth, because that darkness hath blinded his eyes.
@ I write unto you, little children, because ##your##note:x[<<Acts 10:43; 13:38; Eph 1:7>>] sins are forgiven you for his name’s sake.
@ I write unto you, ##fathers##note:x[<<1 John 2:14>>], ##because##note:x[<<John 14:7>>] ##ye have known##note:x[<<John 14:7>>] him __that is__ from the beginning. I write unto you, young men, because ye have overcome the wicked one. I write unto you, little children, because ye have known the Father.
@ I have written unto you, ##fathers##note:x[<<1 John 2:13>>], because ye have known him __that is__ from the beginning. I have written unto you, young men, because ye are strong, and ##the word##note:x[<<Ps 119:11; Col 3:16>>] of God abideth in you, and ye have overcome the wicked one.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Do Not Love the World

@ ##Love not##note:x[<<Rom 12:2>>] the world, neither the things __that are__ in the world. ##If##note:x[<<Matt 6:24; Jas 4:4>>] any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him.
@ For all that __is__ in the world, ##the lust of the flesh##note:x[<<Rom 13:14; Gal 5:17>>], ##and the lust##note:x[<<Ps 119:36–37>>] of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.
@ And ##the world##note:x[<<Matt 24:35; 1 Cor 7:31>>] passeth away, and the lust thereof: ##but##note:x[<<Rom 12:2>>] he that doeth the will of God abideth for ever.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Beware of Antichrists

@ Little children, it is the last time: and as ##ye have##note:x[<<1 John 4:3>>] heard that ##antichrist##note:x[<<1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7>>] shall come, even now are there many antichrists; whereby we know that it is the last time.
@ They ##went out##note:x[<<Acts 20:30; Jude 1:19>>] from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would __no doubt__ have continued with us: but __they went out__, that ##they might##note:x[<<1 Cor 11:19>>] be made manifest that they were not all of us.
@ But ##ye have##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:21–22; 1 John 2:27>>] an unction from the Holy One, ##and ye##note:x[<<John 14:26>>] know all things.
@ I have not written unto you ##because ye know not##note:x[<<Prov 1:5; 9:8–9; 2 Pet 1:12>>] the truth, but because ye know it, and that no lie is of the truth.
@ Who is a liar but ##he that##note:x[<<1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7>>] denieth that Jesus is the Christ? ##He is##note:x[<<1 John 2:18>>] antichrist, that denieth the Father and the Son.
@ Whosoever ##denieth##note:x[<<John 5:23; 8:19; 1 John 4:15>>] the Son, the same hath not the Father: __{startsb}but{endsb}__ __he that acknowledgeth__ __the Son__ __hath__ __the Father__ __also__.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Remain in Christ

@ Let that therefore abide in you, which ye have heard from the beginning. If that which ye have heard from the beginning shall remain in you, ##ye also##note:x[<<John 14:23; 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13>>] shall continue in the Son, and in the Father.
@ ##And this is the promise that he hath promised us, __even__ eternal life.##note:x[<<John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21>>]
@ These __things__ have I written unto you ##concerning##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:1–3; 1 John 3:7; 2 John 1:7>>] them that seduce you.
@ But ##the anointing##note:x[<<1 John 2:20>>] which ye have received of him abideth in you, ##and ye##note:x[<<John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21>>] need not that any man teach you: but as the same anointing teacheth you of all things, and is truth, and is no lie, and even as it hath taught you, ye shall abide in him.
@ And now, little children, abide in him; that, ##when##note:x[<<Mark 8:38>>] he shall appear, we may ##have##note:x[<<1 John 4:17>>] confidence, and not be ashamed before him ##at his##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:13>>] coming.
@ If ye know that he is righteous, ye know ##that every##note:x[<<1 John 3:7>>] one that doeth righteousness ##is born##note:x[<<John 3:3–5; 1 John 5:1>>] of him.

==== Overcoming the World

@ Whosoever ##believeth##note:x[<<John 1:12–13>>] that Jesus is the Christ ##is born##note:x[<<1 John 2:29; 4:7>>] of God: and every one that loveth him that begat loveth him also that is begotten of him.
@ ##By this we know that we love the children of God, when we love God, and keep his commandments.##note:x[<<John 13:34–35; 1 John 3:22–24; 4:21>>]
@ For ##this##note:x[<<Deut 7:9; John 14:15; 15:10>>] is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.
@ For whatsoever is born of God ##overcometh##note:x[<<John 16:33; 1 Cor 15:57; 1 John 4:4>>] the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, __even__ our faith.
@ Who is he that overcometh the world, ##but##note:x[<<1 John 4:15; 5:1>>] he that believeth that Jesus is the Son of God?
@ This is he that came by water and ##blood##note:x[<<Heb 9:14>>], __even__ Jesus Christ; not by water only, but by water and blood. And it is ##the Spirit that##note:x[<<John 15:26>>] beareth witness, because the Spirit ##is truth##note:x[<<John 16:13>>].
@ For there are three that bear record in heaven, ##the Father##note:x[<<Matt 28:19; 2 Cor 13:14>>], the Word, and the Holy Ghost: ##and these##note:x[<<Matt 28:19>>] three are one.
@ And there are three that bear witness in earth, ##the Spirit##note:x[<<John 15:26; 1 John 5:6>>], and the water, and ##the blood##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:21>>]: and these three agree in one.

==== God’s Testimony about His Son

@ If ##we##note:x[<<John 8:17–19; Acts 17:31>>] receive the witness of men, the witness of God is greater: ##for##note:x[<<Matt 3:16–17>>] this is the witness of God which he hath testified of his Son.
@ He that believeth on the Son of God ##hath the##note:x[<<Rom 8:16; Gal 4:6>>] witness in himself: he that believeth not God ##hath made##note:x[<<John 3:33>>] him a liar; because he believeth not the record that God gave of his Son.
@ And this is the record, that ##God##note:x[<<John 3:36; Rom 6:23; 1 John 2:25>>] hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.
@ He ##that hath the##note:x[<<John 3:36; 5:24>>] Son hath life; ##__and__ he##note:x[<<John 3:36>>] that hath not the Son of God hath not life.

==== Effective Prayer

@ These things ##have I##note:x[<<John 20:31>>] written unto you that ##believe##note:x[<<John 1:12; 1 John 3:23>>] on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.
@ And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, ##if##note:x[<<Jer 29:12–13; 33:3; John 14:13>>] we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us:
@ And ##if##note:x[<<Prov 15:29>>] ##we know##note:x[<<Mark 11:24; Luke 11:9–10>>] that he hear us, whatsoever we ask, we know that we have the petitions that we desired of him.
@ If any man see his brother sin a sin __which is__ not unto death, he shall ask, and he shall give him life for them that sin not unto death. ##There##note:x[<<1 Sam 2:25; Heb 6:4–6>>] is a sin unto death: ##I do not##note:x[<<Jer 11:14>>] say that he shall pray for it.
@ ##All##note:x[<<1 John 3:4>>] unrighteousness is sin: ##and##note:x[<<Jas 1:15; 1 John 5:16>>] there is a sin not unto death.

==== The True God

@ We know that ##whosoever##note:x[<<1 John 3:9; 5:1, 4>>] is born of God sinneth not; but he that is begotten of God keepeth himself, and that wicked one toucheth him not.
@ __And__ ##we know##note:x[<<1 John 4:4–6>>] that we are of God, ##and the##note:x[<<1 John 4:4–5>>] whole world lieth ##in wickedness##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:4>>].
@ And we know that the Son of God is come, ##and hath##note:x[<<Luke 24:45; John 17:3>>] given us an understanding, that we may know ##him that##note:x[<<John 17:3>>] is true, and we are in him that is true, __even__ in his Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God, and eternal life.
@ Little children, ##keep##note:x[<<Exod 20:3–4; 1 Cor 10:7, 14>>] yourselves from idols. Amen.
