[discrete.pericope]
==== The Supremacy of the Son

[.parallel]
(<<Colossians 1:15–23>>)

@ God, who ##at##note:x[<<2 Pet 1:20–21>>] sundry times and ##in##note:x[<<Num 12:6–8; Joel 2:28>>] divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets,
@ Hath in these last days spoken unto us by __his__ Son, whom he hath ##appointed##note:x[<<1 Cor 8:6>>] heir of all things, ##by whom##note:x[<<John 1:3; 1 Cor 8:6>>] also he made the worlds;
@ Who being ##the brightness##note:x[<<John 14:9–10>>] of __his__ glory, and the express ##image##note:x[<<Col 1:15–16>>] of his person, and ##upholding##note:x[<<Rev 4:11>>] all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high;
@ Being made ##so##note:x[<<Eph 1:21; 1 Pet 3:22>>] much better than the angels, as he hath ##by##note:x[<<Phil 2:9–11>>] inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they.
@ For unto which of the angels said he at any time, ##Thou##note:x[<<Ps 2:7; Acts 13:33; Heb 5:5>>] art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?
@ And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, he saith, ##And let##note:x[<<Deut 32:43; Ps 97:7; 1 Pet 3:22>>] all the angels of God worship him.
@ And of the angels he saith, ##Who##note:x[<<2 Kgs 6:17; Ps 104:4; Heb 1:14>>] maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire.
@ But unto the Son __he saith__, ##Thy throne##note:x[<<Ps 45:6–7>>], ##O God##note:x[<<Isa 9:6–7; Rom 9:5>>], __is__ for ever and ever: a sceptre of righteousness __is__ the sceptre of thy kingdom.
@ Thou hast ##loved##note:x[<<Ps 45:7>>] righteousness, and hated iniquity; therefore God, __even__ thy God, hath anointed thee with the ##oil##note:x[<<Ps 23:5; Isa 61:3>>] of gladness above thy fellows.
@ And, ##Thou##note:x[<<Ps 102:25–27>>], Lord, ##in##note:x[<<Gen 1:1; John 1:1–3>>] the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands:
@ They ##shall perish##note:x[<<Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33>>]; but thou remainest; and they all ##shall wax##note:x[<<Isa 51:6>>] old as doth a garment;
@ And as a vesture shalt thou fold them up, and they shall be changed: ##but##note:x[<<Heb 13:8; Jas 1:17>>] thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail.
@ But to which of the angels said he at any time, ##Sit##note:x[<<Ps 110:1; Mark 12:36; Heb 10:12>>] on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool?
@ Are they not all ##ministering##note:x[<<Ps 103:20–21, 20–21>>] spirits, sent forth to ##minister##note:x[<<Ps 34:7>>] for them who shall be heirs of salvation?

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Sabbath Rest

[.parallel]
(<<Genesis 2:1–3; Exodus 16:22–30>>)

@ Let ##us therefore##note:x[<<Prov 28:14; Heb 12:15>>] fear, lest, ##a promise##note:x[<<Heb 4:9>>] being left __us__ of entering into his rest, any of you should seem to come short of it.
@ For unto us was the gospel ##preached##note:x[<<Rom 10:16–17>>], as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, ##not being##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:13; Heb 11:6>>] mixed with faith in them that heard __it__.
@ For ##we##note:x[<<Matt 11:28–29>>] which have believed do enter into rest, as he said, ##As I##note:x[<<Ps 95:11>>] have sworn in my wrath, if they shall enter into my rest: although the works were finished ##from##note:x[<<1 Pet 1:20>>] the foundation of the world.
@ For he spake in a certain place of the seventh __day__ on this wise, And ##God##note:x[<<Gen 2:1–2; Exod 20:11; 31:17>>] did rest the seventh day from all his works.
@ And in this __place__ again, If they shall enter into my rest.
@ Seeing therefore it remaineth that ##some##note:x[<<Matt 21:43; 22:9–10>>] must enter there in, and they to whom it was first preached ##entered##note:x[<<Heb 3:18–19>>] not in because of unbelief:
@ Again, he limiteth a certain day, ##saying##note:x[<<Heb 3:7–8>>] in David, ##To day##note:x[<<Ps 95:7; Heb 3:7>>], after so long a time; as it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.
@ For if Jesus ##had##note:x[<<Deut 12:9; Josh 1:15; 22:4>>] given them rest, then would he not afterward have spoken of another day.
@ There ##remaineth##note:x[<<Rev 7:14–17; 21:4>>] therefore a rest to the ##people##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:10>>] of God.
@ For ##he that##note:x[<<Rev 14:13>>] is entered into his rest, he also ##hath##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:1–2>>] ceased from his own works, ##as##note:x[<<Heb 4:3–4>>] God __did__ from his.
@ ##Let##note:x[<<Matt 11:28–30; Luke 13:24>>] us labour therefore to enter into that rest, ##lest##note:x[<<Heb 3:18–19>>] any man fall after the same example of unbelief.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Living Word

[.parallel]
(<<2 Timothy 3:10–17>>)

@ For the word of God ##__is__ quick##note:x[<<Isa 55:11; Jer 23:29>>], and powerful, and ##sharper##note:x[<<Eph 6:17>>] than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and __is__ a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.
@ Neither ##is there##note:x[<<Ps 33:13–15; Prov 15:3; 1 Cor 4:5>>] any creature that is not manifest in his sight: but all things __are__ naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do.
@ Seeing then that we have a great high priest, ##that is##note:x[<<Heb 9:12, 24; 12:2>>] passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast __our__ profession.
@ For ##we have##note:x[<<Isa 53:4–5>>] not an high priest which can not be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points ##tempted##note:x[<<Heb 2:17–18>>] like as __we are,__ ##__yet__##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:21>>] without sin.
@ Let us therefore ##come##note:x[<<Eph 3:12; Heb 10:19–23; 13:6>>] bold ly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.

=== @11

[discrete.pericope]
==== Faith and Assurance

[.parallel]
(<<Genesis 1:1–2; John 1:1–5>>)

@ Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, ##the evidence##note:x[<<Rom 8:24–25; 2 Cor 4:18; Heb 11:7>>] of things not seen.
@ For by it the elders obtained a good report.
@ Through ##faith##note:x[<<Ps 33:6; John 1:3; 2 Pet 3:5>>] we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that things which are seen were not made of things which do appear.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Faith of Abel, Enoch, Noah

[.parallel]
(<<Genesis 4—9>>)

@ By ##faith##note:x[<<Gen 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12>>] Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, by which he obtained witness that he was righteous, God testifying of his gifts: ##and by##note:x[<<Heb 12:24>>] it he being dead yet speaketh.
@ By faith ##Enoch##note:x[<<Jude 1:14>>] was ##translated##note:x[<<2 Kgs 2:11>>] ##that he##note:x[<<Heb 11:6>>] should not see death; and was not found, because God had translated him: for before his translation he had this testimony, that he pleased God.
@ But without faith __it is__ impossible to please __him__: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and __that__ he is a rewarder of them that ##diligently##note:x[<<Prov 8:17; Jer 29:13–14; Matt 6:33>>] seek him.
@ By faith ##Noah##note:x[<<Gen 6:13>>], being ##warned##note:x[<<Gen 6:13>>] of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, ##prepared##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:20>>] an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Faith of Abraham and Sarah

[.parallel]
(<<Genesis 15—22; Romans 4:1–12>>)

@ By faith ##Abraham##note:x[<<Gen 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4>>], when he was called to go out into a place which he should after receive for an inheritance, ##obeyed##note:x[<<Matt 7:24–25>>]; and he went out, not knowing whither he went.
@ By faith he sojourned in the land of promise, as __in__ a strange country, ##dwelling##note:x[<<Gen 12:8; 13:3, 18>>] in tabernacles with Isaac and Jacob, the heirs with him of the same promise:
@ For ##he looked##note:x[<<Phil 3:20; Heb 13:14>>] for a city which hath foundations, ##whose##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:1>>] builder and maker __is__ God.
@ Through faith also ##Sara##note:x[<<Gen 18:11–14>>] herself received strength to conceive seed, and was delivered of a child when she was past age, ##because##note:x[<<Rom 4:20–21; Heb 10:23>>] she judged him faithful who had promised.
@ Therefore sprang there even of one, and him as good as dead, __so many__ ##as the stars##note:x[<<Gen 22:17>>] of the sky in multitude, and ##as the sand##note:x[<<Gen 22:17; 32:12>>] which is by the sea shore innumerable.
@ These all died in faith, not having received the promises, ##but##note:x[<<Heb 11:27>>] having seen them afar off, and were persuaded of __them__, and embraced __them__, and ##confessed##note:x[<<Gen 23:4; Ps 39:12>>] that they were strangers and pilgrims on the earth.
@ For they that say such things declare plainly that ##they seek##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:18>>] a country.
@ And truly, if they had been ##mindful##note:x[<<Gen 24:6–8>>] of that __country__ from whence they came out, they might have had opportunity to have returned.
@ But now they desire a better __country__, that is, an heavenly: wherefore God is not ashamed to be called their God: ##for##note:x[<<Phil 3:20; Heb 11:10; 13:14>>] he hath prepared for them a city.
@ By ##faith##note:x[<<Gen 22:1–12; Jas 2:21–24>>] Abraham, when he was tried, offered up Isaac: and he that had received the promises offered up his ##only##note:x[<<Gen 22:16>>] begotten __son__,
@ Of whom it was said, ##That##note:x[<<Gen 17:19; 21:12; Rom 9:7>>] in Isaac shall thy seed be called:
@ Accounting that ##God##note:x[<<Matt 9:28; Rom 4:17–21; Eph 3:20>>] __was__ able to raise __him__ up, even from the dead; from whence also he received him in a figure.

[.parallel]
(<<Exodus 2—15; Acts 7:20–22>>)

@ note:x[<<Exod 1:16, 22>>]By ##faith##note:x[<<Exod 2:2–10>>] Moses, when he was born, was hid three months of his parents, because they saw __he was__ a proper child; and they were not afraid of the king’s commandment.
