[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Message by the Spirit’s Power

@ And I, brethren, when I came to you, came not ##with##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17; 2:4, 13>>] excellency of speech or of wisdom, declaring unto you the testimony of God.
@ For I determined ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:22–25; Gal 6:14; Phil 3:8–10>>] to know any thing among you, save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.
@ ##And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:10–13; 2 Cor 12:5–10; 13:4>>]
@ And my speech and my preaching __was__ ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17>>] with enticing words of man’s wisdom, ##but##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:20; 1 Thess 1:5>>] in demonstration of the Spirit and of power:
@ That your faith should not stand in the wisdom of men, ##but##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17; 2 Cor 4:7; 6:7>>] in the power of God.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Spiritual Wisdom

[.parallel]
(<<Ephesians 1:15–23>>)

@ Howbeit we speak wisdom among ##them##note:x[<<Heb 5:14>>] that are perfect: yet ##not##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:4; Jas 3:15>>] the wisdom of this world, nor of the princes of this world, that come to nought:
@ But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, ##__even__##note:x[<<Rom 16:25–26; Col 1:26–27>>] the hidden __wisdom__, which God ordained before the world ##unto##note:x[<<2 Pet 1:3>>] our glory:
@ Which ##none##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:6>>] of the princes of this world knew: ##for##note:x[<<Acts 13:27>>] had they known __it__, they would not have crucified ##the Lord##note:x[<<Jas 2:1>>] of glory.
@ But as it is written, ##Eye##note:x[<<Isa 64:4>>] hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, ##the things##note:x[<<Ps 31:19>>] which God hath prepared for ##them##note:x[<<Jas 1:12>>] that love him.
@ But ##God##note:x[<<Luke 10:21; Eph 3:5>>] hath revealed __them__ unto us ##by##note:x[<<John 14:26>>] his Spirit: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God.
@ For ##what##note:x[<<Prov 14:10; 20:27>>] man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? ##even##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:10>>] so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God.
@ Now we have received, ##not##note:x[<<Rom 8:5–6>>] the spirit of the world, but the spirit which is of God; ##that##note:x[<<1 John 2:20, 27>>] we might know the things that are freely given to us of God.
@ note:x[<<1 Cor 2:14>>]Which things also we speak, ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17; 2:4>>] in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.
@ But the natural man ##receiveth##note:x[<<Matt 13:11–17; Rom 8:5–8>>] not the things of the Spirit of God: for ##they##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:18>>] are foolishness unto him: neither can he know __them__, because they are spiritually discerned.
@ But he that is spiritual ##judgeth##note:x[<<Prov 28:5; 1 Thess 5:21; Heb 5:14>>] all things, yet he himself is judged of no man.
@ For ##who##note:x[<<Isa 40:13–14; Rom 11:34>>] hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him? ##But##note:x[<<John 16:13–16>>] we have the mind of Christ.

=== @8

[discrete.pericope]
==== Food Sacrificed to Idols

[.parallel]
(<<Ezekiel 14:1–11; Romans 14:13–23>>)

@ note:x[<<1 Cor 8:7>>]Now as touching things offered unto idols, we know that we all have ##knowledge##note:x[<<Isa 5:21; Rom 14:3>>]. Knowledge puffeth up, but charity edifieth.
@ And ##if##note:x[<<Prov 26:12; Gal 6:3; 1 Tim 6:3–4>>] any man think that he knoweth any thing, he knoweth nothing yet as he ought to know.
@ But if any man love God, the same ##is##note:x[<<Exod 33:17; Nah 1:7; Rev 3:8–9>>] known of him.
@ As concerning therefore the eating of those things that are offered in sacrifice unto idols, ##we know##note:x[<<1 Cor 10:19–20>>] that an idol __is__ nothing in the world, and that ##__there is__##note:x[<<Deut 4:39; 6:4>>] none other God but one.
@ For though there be ##that##note:x[<<Jer 2:28; Gal 4:8; 2 Thess 2:4>>] are called gods, whether in heaven or in earth, ( as there be gods many, and lords many,)
@ But to us __there is but__ ##one God##note:x[<<Mal 2:10>>], the Father, ##of whom##note:x[<<Rom 11:36>>] __are__ all things, and we in him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom __are__ all things, ##and we by##note:x[<<John 1:3>>] him.
@ Howbeit __there is__ not in every man that knowledge: for some ##with##note:x[<<Rom 14:14; 1 Cor 8:9–10; 10:28–29>>] conscience of the idol unto this hour eat __it__ as a thing offered unto an idol; and their conscience being weak is defiled.
@ But ##meat##note:x[<<Rom 14:17; 1 Cor 6:13; Heb 13:9>>] commendeth us not to God: for neither, if we eat, are we the better; neither, if we eat not, are we the worse.
@ note:x[<<Gal 5:13>>]But ##take##note:x[<<Rom 14:20–21; Gal 5:13>>] heed lest by any means this liberty of yours become a stumblingblock to them that are weak.
@ For if any man see thee which hast knowledge ##sit##note:x[<<1 Cor 10:20–21>>] at meat in the idol’s temple, ##shall not##note:x[<<Rom 14:23; 1 Cor 10:28–29>>] the conscience of him which is weak be emboldened to eat those things which are offered to idols;
@ And through thy knowledge ##shall##note:x[<<Rom 14:15; 15:1–3; 1 Cor 8:13>>] the weak brother perish, for whom Christ died?
@ But when ye sin so against the brethren, and wound their weak conscience, ##ye sin against##note:x[<<Matt 25:45>>] Christ.
@ Wherefore, ##if meat##note:x[<<Rom 14:21; 1 Cor 6:12; 2 Cor 11:29>>] make my brother to offend, I will eat no flesh while the world standeth, lest I make my brother to offend.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Warnings from Israel’s Past

[.parallel]
(<<Numbers 16:41–50; Numbers 25:1–5>>)

@ Moreover, brethren, I would not that ye should be ignorant, how that all our fathers were under the cloud, ##and all##note:x[<<Exod 14:29; Neh 9:11; Ps 78:13>>] passed through the sea;
@ ##And were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea;##note:x[<<Exod 14:31; 1 Cor 1:13–16; Heb 3:2–3>>]
@ ##And did all eat the same spiritual meat;##note:x[<<Exod 16:4; Deut 8:3; Neh 9:15>>]
@ And ##did##note:x[<<Exod 17:6; Isa 48:21; John 7:37>>] all drink the same spiritual drink: for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ.
@ ##But with many of them God was not well pleased: for they were overthrown in the wilderness.##note:x[<<Num 26:64–65; Heb 3:17; Jude 1:5>>]
@ Now ##these##note:x[<<1 Cor 10:11; 2 Pet 2:6; Jude 1:7>>] things were our examples, to the intent we should not lust after evil things, as they also lusted.
@ Neither ##be##note:x[<<Ps 106:19–20; 1 Cor 6:9>>] ye idolaters, as __were__ some of them; as it is written, ##The people##note:x[<<Exod 32:6–8>>] sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.
@ ##Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed, and fell in one day three and twenty thousand.##note:x[<<Num 25:1–9; Ps 106:29; 1 Cor 6:18>>]
@ Neither let us ##tempt##note:x[<<Num 21:5; Ps 78:18; Heb 3:8–11>>] Christ, as some of them also tempted, and were destroyed of serpents.
@ Neither ##murmur##note:x[<<Num 14:2; 16:41>>] ye, as some of them also murmured, and ##were##note:x[<<Num 14:37>>] destroyed of the destroyer.
@ Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and ##they##note:x[<<Rom 15:4; 1 Cor 9:10>>] are written for our admonition, ##upon##note:x[<<Phil 4:5>>] whom the ends of the world are come.
@ ##Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall.##note:x[<<Prov 16:18; Rom 11:20; 1 Cor 8:2>>]
@ There hath no temptation taken you ##but##note:x[<<2 Thess 3:3>>] such as is common to man: but God __is__ faithful, ##who##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:18; 2 Pet 2:9>>] will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear __it.__

[discrete.pericope]
==== Flee from Idolatry

[.parallel]
(<<Exodus 20:22–26>>)

@ Wherefore, ##my##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:1>>] dearly beloved, ##flee##note:x[<<1 John 5:21; Rev 21:8>>] from idolatry.
@ ##I speak as to wise men; judge ye what I say.##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:21>>]
@ The ##cup##note:x[<<Matt 26:26–28; 1 Cor 11:23–29>>] of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? ##The bread##note:x[<<1 Cor 11:23–24>>] which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?
@ For ##we __being__##note:x[<<Rom 12:5; 1 Cor 12:12; Col 3:15>>] many are one bread, __and__ one body: for we are all partakers of that one bread.
@ Behold ##Israel##note:x[<<Gal 6:16; Eph 2:11–12; Phil 3:3–5>>] after the flesh: are not they which eat of the sacrifices partakers of the altar?
@ What say I then? ##that the##note:x[<<1 Cor 8:4>>] idol is any thing, or that which is offered in sacrifice to idols is any thing?
@ But I __say__, that the things which the Gentiles ##sacrifice##note:x[<<Deut 32:16–17; Ps 106:37–39; Rev 9:20>>], they sacrifice to devils, and not to God: and I would not that ye should have fellowship with devils.
@ Ye ##cannot drink##note:x[<<1 Kgs 18:21; Matt 6:24; 2 Cor 6:15–17>>] the cup of the Lord, and the cup of devils: ye cannot be partakers of the Lord’s table, and of the table of devils.
@ Do ##we provoke##note:x[<<Deut 32:16, 21>>] the Lord to jealousy? ##are##note:x[<<Heb 10:31>>] we stronger than he?
