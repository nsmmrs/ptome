== @2CO

[.book-title]
The Second Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Corinthians

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul Greets the Corinthians

[.parallel]
(<<Acts 18:1–11; 1 Corinthians 1:1–3>>)

@ ##Paul##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:1>>], an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and ##Timothy##note:x[<<Phil 1:1>>] __our__ brother, unto the church of God which is at Corinth, with ##all##note:x[<<Eph 1:1>>] the saints which are in all Achaia:
@ ##Grace __be__ to you and peace from God our Father, and __from__ the Lord Jesus Christ.##note:x[<<Rom 1:7; Eph 6:23; Phil 1:2>>]

[discrete.pericope]
==== The God of All Comfort

@ ##Blessed##note:x[<<Eph 1:3; 1 Pet 1:3>>] __be__ God, even ##the Father of our##note:x[<<Eph 1:3>>] Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort;
@ Who ##comforteth##note:x[<<Isa 51:12; 2 Thess 2:16–17>>] us in all our tribulation, ##that##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:11>>] we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.
@ For ##as##note:x[<<Phil 1:20; 3:10; Col 1:24>>] the sufferings of Christ abound in us, so our consolation also aboundeth by Christ.
@ And ##whether##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:4; 4:15–18; 2 Tim 2:10>>] we be afflicted, __it is__ for your consolation and salvation, which is effectual in the enduring of the same sufferings which we also suffer: or whether we be comforted, __it is__ for your consolation and salvation.
@ And our hope of you __is__ stedfast, knowing, that ##as ye##note:x[<<Rom 8:17–18; 2 Thess 1:4–7; Jas 1:12>>] are partakers of the sufferings, so __shall ye be__ also of the consolation.
@ For we would not, brethren, have you ignorant ##of our##note:x[<<1 Cor 15:32; 2 Cor 4:7–12>>] trouble which came to us in Asia, that we were pressed out of measure, above strength, ##insomuch##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:8>>] that we despaired even of life:
@ But we had the sentence of death in ourselves, ##that##note:x[<<2 Cor 3:5; 4:7>>] we should not trust in ourselves, but ##in God##note:x[<<Heb 11:19>>] which raiseth the dead:
@ ##Who delivered us from so great a death, and doth deliver: in whom we trust that he will yet deliver __us__;##note:x[<<1 Sam 17:37; Ps 34:19; 2 Pet 2:9>>]
@ Ye also ##helping##note:x[<<Acts 12:5; Phil 1:19>>] together by prayer for us, ##that##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:15>>] for the gift __bestowed__ upon us by the means of many persons thanks may be given by many on our behalf.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Change of Plans

@ For our rejoicing is this, the testimony of our conscience, that in simplicity and ##godly##note:x[<<2 Cor 2:17>>] sincerity, ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:13; Jas 3:13–18>>] with fleshly wisdom, but by the grace of God, we have had our conversation in the world, and more abundantly to you-ward.
@ For we write none other things unto you, ##than##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:2; Phlm 1:6>>] what ye read or acknowledge; and I trust ye shall acknowledge even to the end;
@ As also ye have acknowledged us in part, that we are your rejoicing, ##even##note:x[<<Phil 2:16; 1 Thess 2:19–20>>] as ye also __are__ ours ##in the##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:8>>] day of the Lord Jesus.

@ And ##in##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:19>>] this confidence I was minded to come unto you before, ##that##note:x[<<Rom 1:11; 15:29>>] ye might have a second benefit;
@ And to pass by you into Macedonia, ##and to come##note:x[<<1 Cor 16:5–7>>] again out of Macedonia unto you, and of you to be brought on my way toward Judaea.
@ When I therefore was thus minded, did I use lightness? or the things that I purpose, do I purpose ##according##note:x[<<2 Cor 10:2–3>>] to the flesh, that with me there should be ##yea##note:x[<<Matt 5:37; Jas 5:12>>] yea, and nay nay?
@ But ##__as__##note:x[<<John 7:28; Rev 3:7, 14>>] God __is__ true, our word toward you was not yea and nay.
@ note:x[<<Heb 13:8; Rev 1:17>>]For ##the Son##note:x[<<2 John 1:9>>] of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you by us, __even__ by me and Silvanus and Timotheus, was not yea and nay, but in him was yea.
@ For ##all##note:x[<<Gal 3:22; Heb 13:8; 1 John 2:24–25>>] the promises of God in him __are__ yea, and in him Amen, unto the glory of God by us.
@ Now he which ##stablisheth##note:x[<<1 Pet 5:10>>] us with you in Christ, and hath ##anointed##note:x[<<1 John 2:20, 27>>] us, __is__ God;
@ Who hath also ##sealed##note:x[<<Eph 1:13–14; 4:30>>] us, and given ##the earnest##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:5>>] of the Spirit in our hearts.
@ Moreover ##I call##note:x[<<Rom 1:9; Gal 1:20>>] God for a record upon my soul, ##that##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:21>>] to spare you I came not as yet unto Corinth.
@ Not ##for##note:x[<<1 Cor 15:1>>] ##that##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:24–26; 1 Pet 5:3>>] we have dominion over your faith, but are helpers of your joy: for by faith ye stand.

=== @2

[discrete.pericope]
==== Reaffirm Your Love

@ But I determined this with myself, ##that##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:23; 13:10>>] I would not come again to you in heaviness.
@ For if I make you sorry, who is he then that maketh me glad, but the same which is made sorry by me?
@ And I wrote this same unto you, lest, when I came, I should have sorrow from them of whom I ought to rejoice; ##having##note:x[<<2 Cor 8:22; Gal 5:10; Phlm 1:21>>] confidence in you all, that my joy is __the joy__ of you all.
@ For ##out##note:x[<<Lev 19:17–18; Prov 27:5–6>>] of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote unto you with many tears; ##not##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:12>>] that ye should be grieved, but that ye might know the love which I have more abundantly unto you.
@ But if ##any##note:x[<<1 Cor 5:12–13>>] have caused grief, he hath not grieved me, but in part: that I may not overcharge you all.
@ Sufficient to such a man __is__ this punishment, ##which##note:x[<<1 Cor 5:4–5; 1 Tim 5:20>>] __was inflicted__ of many.
@ So that contrariwise ##ye##note:x[<<Gal 6:1–2; Eph 4:32; Col 3:13>>] __ought__ rather to forgive __him__, and comfort __him__, lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.
@ Wherefore I beseech you ##that##note:x[<<Jude 1:22–23>>] ye would confirm __your__ love toward him.
@ For to this end also did I write, that I might know the proof of you, ##whether##note:x[<<2 Cor 10:6; Phlm 1:21>>] ye be obedient in all things.
@ To ##whom ye##note:x[<<Matt 18:18; John 20:23; 2 Cor 5:20>>] forgive any thing, I __forgive__ also: for if I forgave any thing, to whom I forgave __it__, for your sakes __forgave I it__ in the person of Christ;
@ ##Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:3; Eph 6:11–12; 1 Pet 5:8>>]

[discrete.pericope]
==== Triumph in Christ

@ Furthermore, ##when##note:x[<<Acts 16:8>>] I came to Troas to __preach__ Christ’s gospel, ##and a##note:x[<<Acts 14:27; Col 4:3>>] door was opened unto me of the Lord,
@ I had ##no rest##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:5–6>>] in my spirit, because I found not ##Titus##note:x[<<2 Cor 12:18>>] my brother: but taking my leave of them, I went from thence into Macedonia.
@ Now ##thanks##note:x[<<2 Cor 9:15>>] __be__ unto God, ##which##note:x[<<Rom 8:37>>] always causeth us to triumph in Christ, and maketh manifest ##the savour##note:x[<<2 Cor 2:15–16>>] of his knowledge by us in every place.
@ For we are unto God ##a sweet##note:x[<<Ezek 20:41; Eph 5:2>>] savour of Christ, ##in them##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:18>>] that are saved, and in them that perish:
@ To the one __we are__ ##the savour of death##note:x[<<John 9:39; 1 Pet 2:7–8>>] unto death; and to the other the savour of life unto life. And ##who##note:x[<<2 Cor 3:5–6>>] __is__ sufficient for these things?
@ For we are not as many, ##which##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:2>>] ##corrupt##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:2>>] the word of God: ##but as of sincerity##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:2>>], but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Ministers of a New Covenant

@ Do we ##begin##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:12; 10:12; 12:11>>] again to commend ourselves? or need we, as some __others__, epistles of commendation to you, or __letters__ of commendation from you?
@ Ye ##are##note:x[<<1 Cor 9:1–2>>] our epistle written in our hearts, ##known##note:x[<<Rom 1:8; 1 Thess 1:8>>] and read of all men:
@ __Forasmuch as ye are__ manifestly declared to be the epistle of Christ ministered by us, written not with ink, ##but##note:x[<<Jer 31:33; Ezek 11:19; Heb 8:10>>] with the Spirit of the living God; not in tables of stone, but in fleshy tables of the heart.

@ And ##such##note:x[<<2 Cor 2:14; Phil 1:6>>] trust have we through Christ to ##God-ward##note:x[<<Exod 18:19>>]:
@ Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to think any thing as of ourselves; ##but##note:x[<<1 Cor 15:10; 2 Cor 12:9; Phil 4:13>>] our sufficiency __is__ of God;
@ Who also hath made us able ministers of ##the new##note:x[<<Jer 31:31>>] testament; ##not##note:x[<<Rom 7:6>>] of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, ##but the##note:x[<<John 6:63>>] spirit giveth life.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Glory of the New Covenant

[.parallel]
(<<Exodus 34:10–35>>)

@ But if ##the ministration##note:x[<<2 Cor 3:9>>] of death, ##written##note:x[<<Exod 31:18>>] __and__ engraven in stones, was glorious, so that the children of Israel could not stedfastly behold the face of Moses for the glory of his countenance; ##which##note:x[<<Rom 10:4>>] __glory__ was to be done away:
@ How shall not ##the ministration##note:x[<<Isa 59:21; 1 Cor 3:16; Eph 2:18>>] of the spirit be rather glorious?
@ For if ##the ministration of condemnation##note:x[<<Heb 12:18–21>>] __be__ glory, much more doth ##the ministration of righteousness##note:x[<<Rom 3:21–22; 2 Cor 5:21>>] exceed in glory.
@ For even that which was made glorious ##had##note:x[<<Hag 2:7–9; 2 Pet 1:17>>] no glory in this respect, by reason of the glory that excelleth.
@ For ##if##note:x[<<Rom 5:20–21; Heb 7:21–25; 8:13>>] that which is done away __was__ glorious, much more that which remaineth __is__ glorious.
@ Seeing then that we have such hope, we use great ##plainness##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:4; Eph 6:19–20>>] of speech:
@ And not as Moses, ##__which__##note:x[<<Exod 34:33–35>>] put a vail over his face, that the children of Israel could not stedfastly look ##to the##note:x[<<Eph 2:14–15; Col 2:17>>] end of that which is abolished:
@ But ##their##note:x[<<Rom 11:25; 2 Cor 4:3–4>>] minds were blinded: for until this day remaineth the same vail untaken away in the reading of the old testament; ##which __vail__##note:x[<<Acts 26:18>>] is done away in Christ.
@ But even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart.
@ Nevertheless ##when##note:x[<<Exod 34:34>>] it shall turn to the Lord, ##the vail##note:x[<<Isa 25:7; 29:18>>] shall be taken away.
@ Now ##the Lord##note:x[<<John 6:63>>] is that Spirit: and ##where##note:x[<<Rom 8:2; Gal 4:6>>] the Spirit of the Lord __is__, there __is__ liberty.
@ But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass ##the glory##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:6>>] of the Lord, ##are##note:x[<<Rom 12:2; Eph 4:22–24>>] changed into the same image from glory to glory, __even__ as by the Spirit of the Lord.

@ But have renounced the hidden things of dishonesty, ##not##note:x[<<2 Cor 2:17; 1 Thess 2:3–5>>] walking in craftiness, nor handling the word of God deceitfully; but ##by##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:11>>] manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every man’s conscience in the sight of God.
@ But if our gospel be hid, ##it is##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:18; 2 Cor 3:14; 2 Thess 2:9–11>>] hid to them that are lost:

@ For ##we##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:23; 1 Thess 2:5–6>>] preach not ourselves, but ##Christ##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:23>>] Jesus the Lord; and ourselves your servants for Jesus’ sake.
@ For God, ##who##note:x[<<Gen 1:3>>] commanded ##the light##note:x[<<Isa 60:2; 1 Pet 2:9>>] to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to __give__ the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.
