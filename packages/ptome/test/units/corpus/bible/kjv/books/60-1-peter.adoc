The First Epistle General of Peter

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== A Greeting from Peter

[.parallel]
(<<2 Peter 1:1–2>>)

@ Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to ##the##note:x[<<Acts 2:5–11>>] strangers ##scattered##note:x[<<Jas 1:1>>] throughout ##Pontus##note:x[<<Acts 2:5>>], Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,
@ ##Elect##note:x[<<Eph 1:4–5>>] according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through ##sanctification##note:x[<<2 Thess 2:13>>] of the Spirit, unto obedience and ##sprinkling##note:x[<<Heb 12:24>>] of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied.

[discrete.pericope]
==== A Living Hope

@ Blessed __be__ the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which according to his abundant mercy ##hath##note:x[<<1 Pet 1:23; 1 John 5:4>>] begotten us again ##unto##note:x[<<Rom 15:13>>] a lively hope by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,
@ To ##an##note:x[<<Heb 9:15>>] inheritance incorruptible, and ##undefiled##note:x[<<Rev 21:27>>], and that ##fadeth##note:x[<<1 Pet 5:4>>] not away, reserved in heaven for you,
@ Who are ##kept##note:x[<<Rom 8:31–39; Phil 1:6>>] by the power of God ##through##note:x[<<Eph 2:8>>] faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.
@ Wherein ##ye greatly##note:x[<<Jas 1:2>>] rejoice, though now ##for##note:x[<<1 Pet 5:10>>] a season, if need be, ye are in heaviness through ##manifold##note:x[<<Jas 1:2>>] temptations:
@ That ##the trial##note:x[<<Job 23:10; Jas 1:12>>] of your faith, being much more precious than of gold that perisheth, though it be ##tried##note:x[<<Job 23:10>>] with fire, might be found unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ:
@ Whom ##having##note:x[<<John 20:29; 2 Cor 5:7>>] not seen, ye love; in whom, though now ye see __him__ not, yet ##believing##note:x[<<Rom 15:13>>], ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory:
@ ##Receiving the end of your faith, __even__ the salvation of __your__ souls.##note:x[<<Rom 6:22; Jas 1:21>>]
@ Of ##which##note:x[<<Dan 2:44; Matt 13:17; Luke 10:24>>] salvation the prophets have enquired and searched diligently, who prophesied of the grace __that should come__ unto you:
@ Searching what, or what manner of time ##the Spirit##note:x[<<2 Pet 1:21>>] of Christ which was in them did signify, when it testified beforehand ##the sufferings##note:x[<<Isa 52:13–14; Luke 24:25–27>>] of Christ, and the glory that should follow.
@ Unto whom ##it##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:10>>] was revealed, that not unto themselves, but unto us they did minister the things, which are now reported unto you by them that have preached the gospel unto you ##with##note:x[<<John 15:26>>] the Holy Ghost ##sent##note:x[<<John 15:26>>] down from heaven; which things the angels desire to look into.

[discrete.pericope]
==== A Call to Holiness

[.parallel]
(<<Hebrews 12:14–17>>)

@ Wherefore ##gird##note:x[<<Luke 12:35>>] up the loins of your mind, ##be sober##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:7; 5:8>>], and hope to the end for the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ;
@ As obedient children, ##not##note:x[<<Rom 12:2; 1 Pet 4:2–3>>] fashioning yourselves according to the former lusts ##in##note:x[<<Titus 3:3–5>>] your ignorance:
@ But as he which hath called you is holy, ##so##note:x[<<Matt 5:48; 2 Cor 7:1; Heb 12:14>>] be ye holy in all manner of conversation;
@ ##Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy.##note:x[<<Lev 11:44; 19:2; 20:7>>]
@ And if ye call on the Father, ##who##note:x[<<2 Chr 19:7; Acts 10:34–35>>] without respect of persons judgeth according to every man’s work, pass the time of your sojourning __here__ ##in fear##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:1>>]:
@ Forasmuch as ##ye##note:x[<<Ps 49:7–8; 1 Cor 6:20; 7:23>>] know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, __as__ silver and gold, from your vain conversation __received__ by tradition from your fathers;
@ But ##with##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:22–24; 1 John 1:7>>] the precious blood of Christ, ##as##note:x[<<John 1:29>>] of a lamb without blemish and without spot:
@ Who ##verily##note:x[<<Eph 1:4; 2 Tim 1:9–10>>] was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest ##in##note:x[<<Heb 9:26>>] these last times for you,
@ Who ##by##note:x[<<John 12:44>>] him do believe in God, ##that raised##note:x[<<Acts 2:24>>] him up from the dead, and ##gave##note:x[<<Heb 2:9>>] him glory; that your faith and hope might be in God.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Enduring Word

[.parallel]
(<<Isaiah 40:6–8>>)

@ Seeing ye have purified your souls in obeying the truth through the Spirit ##unto##note:x[<<Rom 12:9–10; 1 Tim 1:5; Heb 13:1>>] unfeigned love of the brethren, __see that ye__ love one another with a pure heart fervently:
@ Being ##born##note:x[<<1 Pet 1:3>>] again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, ##by##note:x[<<John 6:63; Heb 4:12>>] the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.
@ For ##all flesh##note:x[<<Ps 103:15; Isa 40:6–8; Jas 4:14>>] __is__ as grass, and all the glory of man as the flower of grass. The grass withereth, and the flower thereof falleth away:
@ But ##the word##note:x[<<Ps 119:89; Isa 40:8; Matt 5:18>>] of the Lord endureth for ever. And this is the word which by the gospel is preached unto you.

=== @2

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Living Stone and Chosen People

[.parallel]
(<<Isaiah 28:14–22; 1 Corinthians 3:10–15; Ephesians 2:19–22>>)

@ Wherefore ##laying##note:x[<<Jas 1:21>>] aside all ##malice##note:x[<<Eph 4:31>>], and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and ##all evil##note:x[<<Eph 4:31>>] speakings,
@ As newborn babes, desire ##the sincere##note:x[<<Heb 5:12–13>>] milk of the word, that ye may ##grow##note:x[<<Eph 4:15; 2 Pet 3:18>>] thereby:
@ ##If so be ye have tasted that the Lord __is__ gracious.##note:x[<<Ps 24:8; 63:5; Heb 6:5–6>>]
@ To whom coming, __as unto__ a living stone, ##disallowed##note:x[<<Ps 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12>>] indeed of men, but chosen of God, __and__ ##precious##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:7>>],
@ Ye ##also##note:x[<<Eph 2:20–22>>], as lively stones, are built up a spiritual house, ##an holy##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:9; Rev 5:10>>] priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God by Jesus Christ.
@ Wherefore also it is contained in the scripture, ##Behold##note:x[<<Isa 28:16; Rom 9:32–33; 1 Pet 2:4>>], I lay in Sion a chief corner stone, elect, precious: and he that believeth on him shall not be confounded.
@ Unto you therefore which believe __he is__ precious: but unto them which be disobedient, ##the stone##note:x[<<Ps 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12>>] which the builders disallowed, the same is made the head of the corner,
@ And ##a stone##note:x[<<Isa 8:14; 1 Cor 1:23>>] of stumbling, and a rock of offence, __even to them__ which stumble at the word, ##being##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:7>>] disobedient: whereunto also they were appointed.
@ But ye __are__ a chosen generation, ##a royal##note:x[<<Exod 19:5–6; Rev 1:6>>] priesthood, an holy nation, a ##peculiar##note:x[<<Deut 7:6>>] people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:
@ Which in time past ##__were__##note:x[<<Hos 1:9–10; Rom 9:25–26>>] not a people, but __are__ now the people of God: which had not ##obtained##note:x[<<Hos 2:23>>] mercy, but now have obtained mercy.

@ Dearly beloved, I beseech __you__ as strangers and pilgrims, ##abstain##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:2; 1 John 2:15–17>>] from fleshly lusts, which ##war##note:x[<<Gal 5:24>>] against the soul;
@ Having your conversation honest among the Gentiles: ##that##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:16>>], whereas they speak against you as evildoers, ##they may##note:x[<<Matt 5:16; Titus 2:7–8>>] by __your__ good works, which they shall behold, glorify God in the day of visitation.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Submission to Authorities

[.parallel]
(<<Romans 13:1–7>>)

@ note:x[<<Rom 13:1–7; 1 Tim 2:1–2; Titus 3:1>>]Submit yourselves to every ordinance of man for the Lord’s sake: whether it be to the king, as supreme;
@ Or unto governors, as unto them that are sent by him ##for the punishment##note:x[<<Rom 13:3–4>>] of evildoers, and for the praise of them that do well.
@ For ##so##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:18>>] is the will of God, that ##with##note:x[<<Titus 2:8; 1 Pet 2:12>>] well doing ye may put to silence the ignorance of foolish men:
@ As ##free##note:x[<<Rom 6:18, 22; Gal 5:13>>], and not using __your__ liberty for a cloke of maliciousness, but as the servants of God.
@ ##Honour##note:x[<<Prov 24:21; Rom 12:10>>] all __men__. Love the brotherhood. ##Fear##note:x[<<Prov 24:21>>] God. Honour the king.
@ Servants, ##__be__##note:x[<<Eph 6:5–7; Col 3:22–25; Titus 2:9–10>>] subject to __your__ masters with all fear; not only to the good and gentle, but also to the froward.
@ For ##this##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:20>>] __is__ thankworthy, if a man ##for conscience##note:x[<<Matt 5:10–12; 1 Pet 3:14–17>>] toward God endure grief, suffering wrongfully.
@ For what glory __is it__, if, ##when##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:19>>] ye be buffeted for your faults, ye shall take it patiently? but if, when ye do well, and suffer __for it__, ye take it patiently, ##this##note:x[<<Matt 5:10–12>>] __is__ ##acceptable##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:19>>] with God.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Christ’s Example of Suffering

[.parallel]
(<<Isaiah 53:1–8>>)

@ For ##even##note:x[<<Matt 16:24>>] hereunto were ye called: because Christ also suffered for us, ##leaving##note:x[<<John 13:15; 1 John 2:6>>] us an example, that ye should follow his steps:
@ Who ##did##note:x[<<Isa 53:9; 2 Cor 5:21; Heb 4:15>>] no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth:
@ Who, ##when he was##note:x[<<Isa 53:7; Heb 12:3>>] reviled, reviled not again; when he suffered, he threatened not; ##but##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:19>>] committed __himself__ to him that judgeth righteously:
@ Who ##his own self##note:x[<<Isa 53:4–6; Matt 8:17>>] bare our sins in his own body on the tree, that we, ##being##note:x[<<Rom 6:11>>] dead to sins, should live unto righteousness: by whose stripes ye were healed.
@ For ##ye##note:x[<<Isa 53:6; Ezek 34:6; Luke 15:4–6>>] were as sheep going astray; but are now returned unto the Shepherd and Bishop of your souls.

=== @3

[discrete.pericope]
==== Wives and Husbands

[.parallel]
(<<Song 1:1–17; Ephesians 5:22–33>>)

@ Likewise, ##ye##note:x[<<Eph 5:22–24, 33; Col 3:18>>] wives, __be__ in subjection to your own husbands; that, if any obey not the word, they also may without the word be won by the conversation of the wives;
@ While they ##behold##note:x[<<Phil 1:27; 1 Pet 1:15; 2:12>>] your chaste conversation __coupled__ with fear.
@ Whose ##adorning let##note:x[<<Rom 12:2; 1 Tim 2:9–10>>] it not be ##that##note:x[<<Isa 3:18–24>>] outward __adorning__ of plaiting the hair, and of wearing of gold, or of putting on of apparel;
@ But __let it be__ ##the hidden##note:x[<<Rom 2:29>>] man of the heart, in that which is not corruptible, __even the ornament__ of ##a meek##note:x[<<Col 3:12>>] and quiet spirit, ##which is in##note:x[<<1 Sam 16:7>>] the sight of God of great price.
@ For after this manner in the old time ##the holy##note:x[<<Prov 31:30; Titus 2:3–4>>] women also, ##who##note:x[<<1 Tim 5:5>>] trusted in God, adorned themselves, being in subjection unto their own husbands:
@ Even ##as Sara##note:x[<<Gen 18:12>>] obeyed Abraham, calling him lord: whose ##daughters##note:x[<<Rom 9:7–9>>] ye are, as long as ye do well, ##and##note:x[<<Isa 57:11>>] are not afraid with any amazement.
@ Likewise, ##ye##note:x[<<Gen 2:23–24; Matt 19:3–9; Col 3:19>>] husbands, dwell with __them__ according to knowledge, giving honour unto the wife, as unto the weaker vessel, and as being heirs together of the grace of life; that your prayers be not hindered.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Turning from Evil

@ Finally, __be ye__ all of one mind, having compassion one of another, ##love as brethren##note:x[<<Rom 12:10; 1 Pet 1:22>>], __be__ pitiful, __be__ ##courteous##note:x[<<Eph 4:31–32>>]:
@ Not ##rendering##note:x[<<Prov 20:22; Rom 12:14, 17>>] evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing; knowing that ye are thereunto called, that ye should inherit a blessing.
@ For ##he##note:x[<<Ps 34:12–16>>] that will love life, and see good days, let him ##refrain##note:x[<<Jas 1:26>>] his tongue from evil, and his lips that they ##speak##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:22>>] no guile:
@ Let him ##eschew##note:x[<<Ps 34:14>>] evil, and do good; let him ##seek##note:x[<<Matt 5:9; Heb 12:14>>] peace, and ensue it.
@ For ##the eyes##note:x[<<Prov 15:3>>] of the Lord __are__ over the righteous, and ##his ears##note:x[<<Prov 15:29; John 9:31>>] __are open__ unto their prayers: but the face of the Lord __is__ against them that do evil.

@ And ##who##note:x[<<Prov 16:7; Rom 8:28>>] __is__ he that will harm you, if ye be ##followers##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:15>>] of that which is good?

[discrete.pericope]
==== Suffering for Righteousness

@ But and ##if##note:x[<<Matt 5:10–12; 1 Pet 2:19–20>>] ye suffer for righteousness’ sake, happy __are ye__: ##and be##note:x[<<Matt 10:28>>] not afraid of their terror, neither be troubled;
@ But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: ##and __be__##note:x[<<Col 4:6; 2 Tim 2:25>>] ready always to __give__ an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you ##with##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:25–26>>] meekness and fear:
@ Having ##a good##note:x[<<Heb 13:18>>] conscience; that, ##whereas##note:x[<<Titus 2:8; 1 Pet 2:12>>] they speak evil of you, as of evildoers, they may be ashamed that falsely accuse your good conversation in Christ.
@ For __it is__ better, ##if##note:x[<<Matt 26:39; 1 Pet 4:19>>] the will of God be so, that ye ##suffer##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:14>>] for well doing, than for evil doing.
@ For ##Christ##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:21; Heb 9:26, 28>>] also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit:
@ ##By which##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:6>>] also he went and preached unto the spirits ##in##note:x[<<Isa 42:7; 61:1>>] prison;
@ Which ##sometime##note:x[<<Gen 6:3>>] were disobedient, when once the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, ##while##note:x[<<Heb 11:7>>] the ark was a preparing, ##wherein##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:5>>] few, that is, eight souls were saved by water.
@ The like figure whereunto __even__ ##baptism##note:x[<<Mark 16:16; Acts 2:38>>] doth also now save us ( not ##the putting##note:x[<<Ezek 36:25–26>>] away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ:
@ Who ##is gone##note:x[<<Heb 9:24>>] into heaven, and ##is on##note:x[<<Heb 10:12; 12:2>>] the right hand of God; angels and authorities and powers being made subject unto him.

@ Forasmuch then as ##Christ##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:18>>] hath suffered ##for##note:x[<<Gal 5:24; Col 3:3–5>>] us in the flesh, arm yourselves likewise with the same mind: for he that hath suffered in the flesh hath ceased from sin;
@ That he ##no##note:x[<<Eph 4:22–24; Titus 3:3–8>>] longer should live the rest of __his__ time in the flesh to the lusts of men, but to ##the will##note:x[<<Rom 6:11>>] of God.
@ For ##the time##note:x[<<1 Cor 6:11>>] past of __our__ life may suffice us ##to have##note:x[<<1 Thess 4:5>>] wrought the will of the Gentiles, when we walked in lasciviousness, lusts, ##excess##note:x[<<Eph 5:18>>] of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries:
@ Wherein they think it strange that ye run not with __them__ to the same excess of riot, ##speaking##note:x[<<Acts 18:6; 1 Pet 2:12; 3:16>>] evil of __you__:

==== Suffering as Christians

@ Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, ##as##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:2–4; 2 Tim 3:12; 1 Pet 5:9>>] though some strange thing happened unto you:
@ But ##rejoice##note:x[<<Rom 5:3; Jas 1:2–3>>], inasmuch as ##ye are##note:x[<<Rom 8:17>>] partakers of Christ’s sufferings; that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.
@ If ##ye be##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:14>>] ##reproached##note:x[<<Matt 5:11; Luke 6:22>>] for the name of Christ, happy __are ye__; for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you: on their part he is evil spoken of, but on your part he is glorified.
@ note:x[<<1 Thess 4:11; 2 Thess 3:11; 1 Tim 5:13>>]But let none of you suffer as a murderer, or __as__ a thief, or __as__ an evildoer, or as a busybody in other men’s matters.
@ Yet if __any man suffer__ ##as##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:17–18; 4:19>>] a Christian, let him not be ashamed; ##but##note:x[<<Phil 1:29>>] let him glorify God on this behalf.
@ For the time __is come__ that ##judgment##note:x[<<Jer 25:29; Ezek 9:6>>] must begin at the house of God: and if __it__ first __begin__ at us, what shall the end __be__ of them that ##obey##note:x[<<2 Thess 1:8>>] not the gospel of God?
@ And ##if##note:x[<<Prov 11:31; Ezek 18:24; Luke 23:31>>] the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?
@ Wherefore ##let##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:17>>] them that suffer according to the will of God ##commit##note:x[<<Ps 37:5; 2 Tim 1:12>>] the keeping of their souls __to him__ in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator.
