== @TIT

[.book-title]
The Epistle of Paul the Apostle to Titus

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Greeting to Titus

[.parallel]
(<<2 Corinthians 8:16–24>>)

@ Paul, ##a servant##note:x[<<Rom 1:1>>] of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of God’s elect, and ##the acknowledging##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:23>>] of the truth which is ##after##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:3>>] godliness;
@ In ##hope##note:x[<<1 John 2:25>>] of eternal life, which ##God##note:x[<<Num 23:19>>], that cannot lie, ##promised##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:9>>] before the world began;
@ But hath in due times manifested his word through preaching, ##which##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:11>>] is committed unto me according to the commandment of ##God##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:1; Titus 3:4–6>>] our Saviour;
@ To Titus, ##__mine__##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:2>>] own son after ##the common##note:x[<<Jude 1:3>>] faith: ##Grace##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:2>>], mercy, __and__ peace, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ our Saviour.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Appointing Elders on Crete

[.parallel]
(<<1 Timothy 3:1–7; 1 Peter 5:1–4>>)

@ For this cause ##left I##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:3>>] thee in Crete, that thou shouldest ##set##note:x[<<Eccl 12:9>>] in order the things that are wanting, ##and##note:x[<<Acts 14:23>>] ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:
@ If ##any##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:2–7>>] be blameless, ##the husband##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:12>>] of one wife, having faithful children ##not##note:x[<<Prov 28:7>>] accused of riot or unruly.
@ For a bishop must be blameless, ##as##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:10>>] the steward of God; ##not selfwilled##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:10>>], not soon angry, ##not given to wine##note:x[<<Eph 5:18>>], no striker, not given to filthy lucre;
@ But ##a lover of hospitality##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:2>>], a lover of good men, ##sober##note:x[<<Titus 2:7>>], just, holy, temperate;
@ ##Holding##note:x[<<2 Thess 2:15; 2 Tim 1:13>>] ##fast##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:3>>] the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Correcting False Teachers

[.parallel]
(<<1 Timothy 1:3–11>>)

@ For ##there##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:6; 1 John 4:1>>] are many unruly and vain talkers and deceivers, ##specially##note:x[<<Gal 2:4>>] they of the circumcision:
@ Whose ##mouths##note:x[<<Titus 3:10>>] must be stopped, who ##subvert##note:x[<<2 Tim 3:6>>] whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for ##filthy##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:5>>] lucre’s sake.
@ One ##of##note:x[<<Acts 17:28>>] themselves, __even__ a prophet of their own, said, The Cretians __are__ alway ##liars##note:x[<<Rom 16:18; 1 Tim 4:2>>], evil beasts, slow bellies.
@ This witness is true. Wherefore ##rebuke##note:x[<<1 Tim 5:20; Titus 2:15>>] them sharply, ##that##note:x[<<Titus 2:2>>] they may be sound in the faith;
@ Not giving heed to ##Jewish##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:4>>] fables, and ##commandments##note:x[<<Col 2:22>>] of men, that ##turn##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:4>>] from the truth.
@ Unto ##the pure##note:x[<<Rom 14:14, 20>>] all things __are__ pure: ##but##note:x[<<Rom 14:20>>] unto them that are defiled and unbelieving __is__ nothing pure; but even their mind and conscience is defiled.
@ They ##profess##note:x[<<Isa 29:13; Ezek 33:31>>] that they know God; but in works they deny __him__, being abominable, ##and disobedient##note:x[<<Eph 5:6>>], and unto every good work reprobate.

=== @2

[discrete.pericope]
==== Teaching Sound Doctrine

@ ##But speak thou the things which become sound doctrine:##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:3; 2 Tim 1:13; Titus 1:9>>]
@ That the aged men be ##sober##note:x[<<1 Pet 4:7>>], grave, ##temperate##note:x[<<Gal 5:23; 1 Pet 4:7>>], sound in faith, in charity, in patience.
@ The aged women likewise, that __they be__ in behaviour ##as##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:11>>] becometh ##holiness##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:11>>], not false accusers, not given to much wine, ##teachers##note:x[<<Titus 2:4>>] of good things;
@ That they may teach ##the##note:x[<<1 Tim 5:2, 14>>] young women to be sober, ##to love their husbands##note:x[<<1 Tim 5:14>>], to love their children,
@ __To be__ discreet, chaste, ##keepers##note:x[<<Prov 31:10–31; 1 Tim 5:13>>] at home, good, ##obedient##note:x[<<Col 3:18>>] to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.
@ ##Young##note:x[<<Eccl 11:9; 12:1; 1 Pet 5:5>>] men likewise exhort to be sober minded.
@ In ##all##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:12; 1 Pet 5:3>>] things shewing thyself a pattern of good works: in doctrine __shewing__ ##uncorruptness##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:2>>], gravity, sincerity,
@ ##Sound##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:3>>] speech, that cannot be condemned; ##that he##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:12, 15>>] that is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say of you.
@ __Exhort__ ##servants##note:x[<<Eph 6:5–8; 1 Tim 6:1–2; 1 Pet 2:18–25>>] to be obedient unto their own masters, __and__ to please __them__ well in all __things__; not answering again;
@ Not purloining, but shewing all good fidelity; that they may ##adorn##note:x[<<Matt 5:16; 1 Pet 2:12>>] ##the doctrine##note:x[<<2 John 1:9>>] of God our Saviour in all things.

[discrete.pericope]
==== God’s Grace Brings Salvation

@ For ##the grace##note:x[<<Eph 2:8>>] of God that bringeth salvation ##hath##note:x[<<Isa 45:22; 1 Tim 2:4>>] appeared to all men,
@ Teaching us that, ##denying##note:x[<<Matt 16:24; Rom 6:19>>] ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should ##live##note:x[<<Ezek 36:27>>] soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;
@ ##Looking##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:7; 2 Tim 4:8>>] for that blessed hope, and ##the glorious##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:8>>] appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ;
@ Who ##gave##note:x[<<Heb 9:14>>] himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and ##purify##note:x[<<Heb 9:14>>] unto himself a peculiar people, ##zealous##note:x[<<Eph 2:10>>] of good works.
@ These things ##speak##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:2; Titus 1:13>>], and exhort, and rebuke with all authority. ##Let##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:12>>] no man despise thee.

=== @3

[discrete.pericope]
==== Heirs of Grace

@ Put them in mind ##to be subject##note:x[<<Eccl 10:4; Rom 13:1–7; 1 Pet 2:13–17>>] to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates, to be ready to every good work,
@ To ##speak##note:x[<<Eph 4:31>>] evil of ##no##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:24–25>>] man, to be no brawlers, __but__ ##gentle##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:24–25>>], shewing all meekness unto all men.
@ For ##we##note:x[<<Eph 2:1–3; Col 1:21>>] ourselves also were sometimes foolish, disobedient, deceived, ##serving##note:x[<<Rom 6:17>>] divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, __and__ hating one another.
@ But after that ##the kindness##note:x[<<Eph 2:4–10>>] and love of ##God##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:10; Titus 2:10>>] our Saviour toward man appeared,
@ Not ##by works##note:x[<<Eph 2:8–9; 2 Tim 1:9>>] of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the ##washing##note:x[<<1 Cor 6:11>>] of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost;
@ Which ##he shed##note:x[<<Joel 2:28; Acts 10:45; Rom 5:5>>] on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Saviour;
@ That ##being##note:x[<<Rom 3:24; Titus 2:11>>] justified by his grace, we should be ##made##note:x[<<Rom 8:17>>] heirs according to the hope of eternal life.
@ __This is__ ##a faithful##note:x[<<Titus 1:9>>] saying, and these things I will that thou affirm constantly, that they which have believed in God might ##be##note:x[<<Titus 2:14; 3:14>>] careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable unto men.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Avoid Divisions

[.parallel]
(<<Romans 16:17–20>>)

@ But ##avoid##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:7; 2 Tim 2:23>>] foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law; for they are ##unprofitable##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:14>>] and vain.
@ A man that is an heretick ##after##note:x[<<Matt 18:15–17>>] the first and second admonition ##reject##note:x[<<Rom 16:17; 2 John 1:10>>];
@ Knowing that he that is such ##is subverted##note:x[<<Heb 10:26>>], and sinneth, ##being##note:x[<<Luke 19:22; John 3:18>>] condemned of himself.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Final Remarks and Greetings

@ When I shall send Artemas unto thee, or ##Tychicus##note:x[<<Acts 20:4>>], ##be##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:9>>] diligent to come unto me to Nicopolis: for I have determined there to winter.
@ Bring Zenas the lawyer and ##Apollos##note:x[<<Acts 18:24>>] on their journey diligently, that nothing be wanting unto them.
@ And let ours also learn to maintain good works for necessary uses, ##that##note:x[<<Matt 7:19; Phil 1:11; Col 1:10>>] they be not unfruitful.
@ All that are with me salute thee. Greet them that ##love##note:x[<<Eph 6:23; 2 John 1:1–2>>] us in the faith. Grace __be__ with you all. Amen.

[.colophon]
It was written to Titus, ordained the first bishop of the church of the Cretians, from Nicopolis of Macedonia.
