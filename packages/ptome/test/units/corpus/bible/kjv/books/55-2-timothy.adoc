== @2TI

[.book-title]
The Second Epistle of Paul the Apostle to Timothy

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Greeting to Timothy

[.parallel]
(<<1 Timothy 1:1–2>>)

@ Paul, ##an##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:1>>] apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, according to ##the promise##note:x[<<John 17:3; Heb 9:15>>] of life which is in Christ Jesus,
@ To ##Timothy##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:2>>], __my__ dearly beloved son: Grace, mercy, __and__ peace, from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Faithfulness under Persecution

[.parallel]
(<<Matthew 10:16–25>>)

@ ##I thank##note:x[<<Rom 1:8>>] God, ##whom##note:x[<<Acts 24:14>>] I serve from __my__ forefathers ##with##note:x[<<Heb 13:8>>] pure conscience, that without ceasing I have remembrance of thee in my prayers night and day;
@ Greatly ##desiring##note:x[<<Phil 1:8; 2 Tim 4:9, 21>>] to see thee, being mindful of thy tears, that I may be filled with joy;
@ When I call to remembrance the ##unfeigned##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:5; 4:6>>] faith that is in thee, which dwelt first in thy grandmother Lois, and ##thy mother##note:x[<<Acts 16:1>>] Eunice; and I am persuaded that in thee also.
@ Wherefore I put thee in remembrance ##that##note:x[<<Rom 12:6–8; 1 Pet 4:10–11>>] thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee ##by the##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:14>>] putting on of my hands.
@ For God hath not given us ##the spirit##note:x[<<Rom 8:15; 1 John 4:18>>] of fear; but of power, and ##of love##note:x[<<Gal 5:22>>], and of a sound mind.
@ Be not thou therefore ##ashamed##note:x[<<Mark 8:38; Rom 1:16>>] of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but ##be thou##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:5>>] partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God;
@ Who ##hath##note:x[<<Eph 2:8; Titus 3:4–5>>] saved us, and called __us__ with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, ##which##note:x[<<Eph 1:3>>] was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began,
@ But is ##now##note:x[<<Rom 16:26; 1 John 1:2>>] made manifest by the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who hath abolished death, ##and hath##note:x[<<1 John 1:2>>] brought life and immortality to light through the gospel:
@ note:x[<<Acts 9:15; Eph 3:7–8; 1 Tim 2:7>>]Where unto I am appointed a preacher, and an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.
@ For ##the which##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:8>>] cause I also suffer these things: nevertheless ##I am##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:8>>] not ashamed: for I know whom I have believed, and am persuaded that he is able to ##keep##note:x[<<Jude 1:24>>] that which I have committed unto him against that day.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Holding to Sound Teaching

@ ##Hold##note:x[<<2 Tim 3:14; Titus 1:9>>] fast the form of sound words, ##which##note:x[<<Phil 4:9>>] thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.
@ That ##good##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:20>>] thing which was committed unto thee keep ##by the##note:x[<<Rom 8:13>>] Holy Ghost ##which dwelleth##note:x[<<John 14:17>>] in us.
@ This thou knowest, that all they which are in Asia ##be##note:x[<<Phil 2:21; 2 Tim 4:16>>] turned away from me; of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.
@ The ##Lord##note:x[<<Heb 10:34>>] give mercy unto ##the house##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:19>>] of Onesiphorus; for he oft ##refreshed##note:x[<<Phlm 1:20>>] me, and was not ashamed of my chain:
@ But, when he was in Rome, he sought me out very diligently, and found __me__.
@ The Lord grant unto him that he may find mercy of the Lord ##in that##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:12>>] day: and in how many things he ##ministered##note:x[<<Heb 6:10>>] unto me at Ephesus, thou knowest very well.

=== @2

[discrete.pericope]
==== Grace and Perseverance

[.parallel]
(<<Hebrews 12:1–3>>)

@ Thou therefore, my son, ##be##note:x[<<2 Cor 12:9–10; Eph 6:10; 2 Tim 1:7>>] strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.
@ And ##the things##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:13>>] that thou hast heard of me among ##many##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:12>>] witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, ##who##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:6>>] shall be able to teach others also.
@ Thou therefore ##endure##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:5; Heb 12:2–3; Jas 1:12>>] hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.
@ No man that warreth ##entangleth##note:x[<<Luke 8:14; 2 Pet 2:20>>] himself with the affairs of __this__ life; ##that he##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:4>>] may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.
@ And if a man also ##strive##note:x[<<1 Cor 9:24–27>>] for masteries, __yet__ ##is he##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:7–8; Rev 3:11>>] not crowned, except he strive lawfully.
@ The ##husbandman##note:x[<<1 Cor 3:6–9; 9:7–11>>] ##that laboureth must be first partaker of the fruits##note:x[<<Heb 10:36>>].
@ Consider what I say; ##and##note:x[<<Gen 41:38–39; Jas 1:5; 1 John 5:20>>] the Lord give thee understanding in all things.
@ Remember that Jesus Christ of the seed of David was ##raised##note:x[<<Acts 2:24; 1 Cor 15:11–20>>] from the dead ##according##note:x[<<Rom 2:16>>] to my gospel:
@ Where in ##I suffer##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:8>>] trouble, as an evil doer, ##__even__##note:x[<<Acts 28:31>>] unto bonds; ##but##note:x[<<Phil 1:7>>] the word of God is not bound.
@ Therefore ##I endure##note:x[<<Col 1:24>>] all things ##for##note:x[<<Col 1:24>>] the elect’s sakes, that they may also obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus ##with##note:x[<<1 Pet 5:10>>] eternal glory.
@ __It is__ a faithful saying: ##For##note:x[<<Rom 6:5, 8; Col 3:3–4>>] if we be dead with __him__, we shall also live with __him__:
@ If ##we suffer##note:x[<<Rom 8:17; Rev 20:4>>], we shall also reign with __him__: ##if we deny##note:x[<<Matt 10:33>>] __him__, he also will deny us:
@ note:x[<<Num 23:19>>]If we believe not, ##__yet__##note:x[<<Rom 3:3; 1 Thess 5:24>>] he abideth faithful: he can not deny himself.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Lord’s Approved Workman

@ Of these things put __them__ in remembrance, ##charging##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:1>>] __them__ before the Lord ##that##note:x[<<2 Tim 2:23; Titus 3:9–11>>] they strive not about words to no profit, __but__ to the subverting of the hearers.
@ ##Study##note:x[<<2 Pet 1:10>>] to shew thyself ##approved##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:4>>] unto God, ##a workman##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:6>>] that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.
@ But ##shun##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:20; 2 Tim 2:14; Titus 3:9>>] profane __and__ vain babblings: for they will increase unto more ungodliness.
@ And ##their word##note:x[<<Nah 3:15>>] will eat as doth a canker: of whom is ##Hymenaeus##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:20>>] and Philetus;
@ Who ##concerning##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:19>>] the truth have erred, saying ##that##note:x[<<1 Cor 15:12>>] the resurrection is past already; and overthrow the faith of some.
@ Nevertheless the foundation of God standeth sure, having this seal, ##The Lord##note:x[<<Nah 1:7; John 10:14; 1 Cor 8:3>>] knoweth them that are his. And, Let every one that nameth the name of Christ depart from iniquity.
@ But ##in a##note:x[<<1 Tim 3:15; 1 Pet 2:5>>] great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; ##and some to honour##note:x[<<Rom 9:21–23>>], and some to dishonour.
@ If a man therefore ##purge##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:1>>] himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master’s use, __and__ ##prepared##note:x[<<Eph 2:10; 2 Tim 3:17>>] unto every good work.
@ ##Flee##note:x[<<1 Cor 6:18>>] also ##youthful##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:11>>] lusts: but follow righteousness, faith, charity, ##peace##note:x[<<1 Pet 3:11>>], with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
@ ##But foolish and unlearned questions avoid, knowing that they do gender strifes.##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:7; 2 Tim 2:14; Titus 3:9>>]
@ And ##the servant##note:x[<<Titus 3:2>>] of the Lord ##must##note:x[<<Jas 1:19–20>>] not strive; ##but##note:x[<<Titus 3:2>>] be gentle unto all __men__, apt to teach, patient,
@ ##In##note:x[<<Gal 6:1>>] meekness instructing those that oppose themselves; ##if##note:x[<<Acts 11:18>>] God ##peradventure##note:x[<<1 Tim 2:4>>] will give them repentance to the acknowledging of the truth;
@ And __that__ they may recover themselves ##out##note:x[<<Ps 124:7; 1 Tim 3:7>>] of the snare of the devil, ##who are##note:x[<<Isa 49:25–26>>] taken captive by him at his will.

=== @3

[discrete.pericope]
==== Evil in the Last Days

@ This know also, that ##in##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:1; 2 Tim 4:3; 2 Pet 3:3>>] the last days perilous times shall come.
@ For men shall be ##lovers##note:x[<<Phil 2:21>>] of their own selves, ##covetous##note:x[<<Rom 1:29>>], ##boasters##note:x[<<Rom 1:29–31>>], proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,
@ note:x[<<Jude 1:18>>]Without ##natural##note:x[<<Rom 1:31>>] affection, ##trucebreakers##note:x[<<Rom 1:31>>], false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good,
@ ##Traitors##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:10–22>>], heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than ##lovers of God##note:x[<<Phil 3:18–19; Jude 1:4>>];
@ Having ##a form##note:x[<<Isa 29:13; Matt 23:27–28; Titus 1:16>>] of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away.
@ For ##of this##note:x[<<Jude 1:4>>] sort are they which creep into houses, and lead captive silly women laden with sins, ##led##note:x[<<2 Pet 3:17>>] away with ##divers##note:x[<<Titus 3:3>>] lusts,
@ Ever ##learning##note:x[<<Eph 4:14; 2 Tim 4:3–4>>], and ##never##note:x[<<1 Tim 2:4>>] able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
@ Now ##as##note:x[<<Exod 7:11>>] Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so do these also ##resist##note:x[<<Acts 13:8–11; Rev 2:20>>] the truth: men of corrupt minds, reprobate concerning the faith.
@ But they shall proceed no further: for ##their##note:x[<<Exod 7:12; Jer 28:15–17; 2 Tim 3:8>>] folly shall be manifest unto all __men__, as theirs also was.

[discrete.pericope]
==== All Scripture Is God-Breathed

[.parallel]
(<<Hebrews 4:12–16>>)

@ But thou hast fully known ##my##note:x[<<Titus 2:7>>] doctrine, manner of life, purpose, ##faith##note:x[<<1 Tim 6:11; 2 Pet 1:5–7>>], longsuffering, charity, patience,
@ ##Persecutions##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:23–28>>], afflictions, which came unto me ##at Antioch##note:x[<<Acts 14:5–6>>], at Iconium, at Lystra; what persecutions I endured: ##but##note:x[<<Ps 34:19>>] out of __them__ all the Lord delivered me.
@ Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus ##shall##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:20–21; 3:14; 4:12–16>>] suffer persecution.
@ But ##evil##note:x[<<2 Thess 2:6–10; 1 Tim 4:1; 2 Tim 3:8>>] men and seducers shall wax worse and worse, deceiving, and being deceived.
@ But ##continue##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:13; 2:2>>] thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, ##knowing##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:13>>] of whom thou hast learned __them__;
@ And that ##from##note:x[<<Prov 22:6>>] a child thou hast known ##the holy##note:x[<<Luke 24:45; 2 Pet 1:20–21>>] scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.
@ ##All##note:x[<<Rom 15:4; Heb 4:12; 2 Pet 1:19–21>>] scripture __is__ given by inspiration of God, and __is__ profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:
@ That the man of God may be perfect, ##throughly furnished##note:x[<<2 Cor 9:8; Eph 2:10; 2 Tim 2:21>>] unto all good works.

=== @4

[discrete.pericope]
==== Preach the Word

@ I ##charge##note:x[<<1 Tim 5:21>>] __thee__ therefore before God, and the Lord Jesus Christ, ##who##note:x[<<Acts 10:42>>] shall judge the quick and the dead ##at##note:x[<<2 Tim 4:8>>] his appearing and his kingdom;
@ Preach the word; ##be##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:15–16>>] instant in season, out of season; ##reprove##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:14; Rev 3:19>>], rebuke, exhort with all longsuffering and doctrine.
@ For ##the time##note:x[<<1 Tim 4:1–3; 2 Tim 3:1–6>>] will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; ##but##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:1–3>>] after their own lusts shall they heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears;
@ And they shall ##turn##note:x[<<Zech 7:11; Acts 7:57>>] away __their__ ears from the truth, and shall be turned ##unto##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:4>>] fables.
@ But watch thou in all things, ##endure##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:8; 2:3>>] afflictions, do the work of an evangelist, ##make full proof of##note:x[<<Col 4:17>>] thy ministry.
@ For ##I am##note:x[<<Phil 2:17>>] now ready to be offered, ##and##note:x[<<Josh 23:14; Phil 1:23>>] the time of my departure is at hand.
@ note:x[<<1 Tim 6:12>>]I have fought a good fight, ##I have finished##note:x[<<1 Cor 9:24–27; Heb 12:1–2>>] __my__ course, I have kept the faith:
@ Henceforth there is laid up for me ##a crown##note:x[<<Jas 1:12; 1 Pet 5:4; Rev 2:10>>] of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous judge, shall give me at that day: and not to me only, but unto all them also that love his appearing.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Personal Concerns

@ ##Do thy diligence to come shortly unto me:##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:4; 4:21>>]
@ For ##Demas##note:x[<<Col 4:14–15>>] ##hath##note:x[<<2 Tim 1:15>>] forsaken me, ##having##note:x[<<1 John 2:15–16>>] loved this present world, and is departed unto Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus unto Dalmatia.
@ Only ##Luke##note:x[<<Phlm 1:24>>] is with me. Take ##Mark##note:x[<<Acts 12:12, 25>>], and bring him with thee: for he is profitable to me for the ministry.
@ And ##Tychicus##note:x[<<Acts 20:4; Eph 6:21>>] have I sent to Ephesus.
@ The cloke that I left at Troas with Carpus, when thou comest, bring __with thee__, and the books, __but__ especially the parchments.
@ ##Alexander##note:x[<<1 Tim 1:20>>] the coppersmith did me much evil: the Lord ##reward##note:x[<<Ps 28:4; 2 Thess 1:6>>] him according to his works:
@ Of whom be thou ware also; for he hath greatly ##withstood##note:x[<<2 Tim 3:8>>] our words.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Lord Remains Faithful

@ At my first answer ##no##note:x[<<Mark 14:50; John 16:32>>] man stood with me, but all __men__ forsook me: ##__I pray__##note:x[<<Acts 7:60>>] __God__ that it may not be laid to their charge.
@ Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me, and strengthened me; that ##by##note:x[<<Phil 1:12–14>>] me the preaching might be fully known, and __that__ all the Gentiles might hear: ##and I##note:x[<<Ps 22:21; 1 Pet 5:8>>] was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.
@ And the Lord shall ##deliver##note:x[<<Ps 121:7; 2 Thess 3:3>>] me from every evil work, ##and will##note:x[<<Jude 1:24>>] preserve __me__ unto his heavenly kingdom: to whom __be__ glory for ever and ever. Amen.
