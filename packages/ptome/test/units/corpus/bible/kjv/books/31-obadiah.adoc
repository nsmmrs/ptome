==== The Destruction of Edom

[.parallel]
(<<Jeremiah 49:7–22>>)

@ The vision of Obadiah. Thus saith the Lord GOD ##concerning##note:x[<<Ezek 25:12–14; Joel 3:19; Amos 1:11–12>>] Edom; We have heard a rumour from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent among the heathen, Arise ye, and let us rise up against her in battle.
@ ##Behold, I have made thee small among the heathen: thou art greatly despised.##note:x[<<Num 24:18; Isa 23:9; Luke 1:51–52>>]

@ ¶ The ##pride##note:x[<<Prov 29:23; Jer 49:16>>] of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation __is__ high; that ##saith##note:x[<<Isa 14:13–15>>] in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?
@ Though thou ##exalt##note:x[<<Job 20:6–7; Hab 2:9>>] __thyself__ as the eagle, and though thou set thy nest ##among##note:x[<<Amos 9:2>>] the stars, thence will I bring thee down, saith the LORD.
@ If thieves came to thee, ##if robbers##note:x[<<Jer 49:9>>] by night, (how art thou cut off!) would they not have stolen till they had enough? ##if the##note:x[<<Deut 24:21>>] grapegatherers came to thee, would they not leave __some__ grapes?note:tn[__some…__: or, gleanings?]
@ How ##are __the__##note:x[<<Jer 49:10>>] __things__ of Esau searched out! __how__ are his hidden things sought up!
@ All ##the men of##note:x[<<Jer 30:14>>] thy confederacy have brought thee __even__ to the border: the men that were at peace with thee have deceived thee, __and__ prevailed against thee; ##__they that eat__ thy bread##note:x[<<Ps 41:9; John 13:18>>] have laid a wound under thee: __there is__ none understanding ##in him##note:tn[or, of it].note:tn[__that were…__: Heb. of thy peace]note:tn[__they…__: Heb. the men of thy bread]
@ Shall I not in that day, saith the LORD, ##even##note:x[<<Job 5:12–14; Ps 33:10; Isa 29:14>>] destroy the wise __men__ out of Edom, and understanding out of the mount of Esau?
@ And thy mighty __men__, ##O##note:x[<<Ezek 25:13; Amos 1:12>>] Teman, shall be dismayed, to the end that ##every##note:x[<<Isa 63:1–3>>] one of the mount of Esau may be cut off by slaughter.

@ ¶ For __thy__ ##violence##note:x[<<Gen 27:41; Amos 1:11>>] against thy brother Jacob shame shall cover thee, ##and##note:x[<<Mal 1:3–4>>] thou shalt be cut off for ever.
@ In the day that thou stoodest on the other side, in the day that the strangers carried away captive his forces, and foreigners entered into his gates, and ##cast##note:x[<<Joel 3:3; Nah 3:10>>] lots upon Jerusalem, ##even##note:x[<<Ps 137:7>>] thou __wast__ as one of them.note:tn[__captive…__: or, his substance]
@ But thou shouldest not have ##looked##note:x[<<Mic 4:11>>] on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have ##rejoiced##note:x[<<Prov 17:5; Ezek 35:15>>] over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly in the day of distress.note:tn[__spoken…__: Heb. magnified thy mouth]
@ Thou shouldest not have entered into the gate of my people in the day of their calamity; yea, thou shouldest not have ##looked##note:x[<<Zech 1:15>>] on their affliction in the day of their calamity, nor have laid __hands__ on their ##substance##note:tn[or, forces] in the day of their calamity;
@ Neither shouldest thou have stood in the crossway, to cut off those of his that did escape; neither shouldest thou have delivered up those of his that did remain in the day of distress.note:tn[__delivered up__: or, shut up]

==== The Deliverance of Israel

@ For the day of the LORD __is__ near upon all the heathen: ##as##note:x[<<Hab 2:8; Matt 7:2; Jas 2:13>>] thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee: thy reward shall return upon thine own head.
@ For ##as ye##note:x[<<Jer 49:12; Joel 3:17>>] have drunk upon my holy mountain, __so__ shall all the heathen drink continually, yea, they shall drink, and they shall swallow down, and they shall be as though they had not been.note:tn[__swallow…__: or, sup up]

@ ¶ But upon mount Zion shall be ##deliverance##note:tn[or, they that escape], and ##there shall be holiness##note:x[<<Rev 21:27>>]; and the house of Jacob shall ##possess##note:x[<<Isa 14:1–2; Amos 9:11–15>>] their possessions.note:tn[__there…__: or, it shall be holy]
@ And the house of Jacob ##shall be##note:x[<<Isa 10:17; Mic 5:8; Zech 12:6>>] a fire, and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau for stubble, and they shall kindle in them, and devour them; and there shall not be __any__ remaining of the house of Esau; for the LORD hath spoken __it__.
@ And __they of__ ##the south##note:x[<<Jer 32:44; Amos 9:12>>] shall possess the mount of Esau; and __they of__ ##the plain##note:x[<<Zeph 2:4–7>>] the Philistines: and they shall possess the fields of Ephraim, and the fields of Samaria: and Benjamin __shall possess__ Gilead.
@ And the captivity of this host of the children of Israel __shall possess__ that of the Canaanites, __even__ unto ##Zarephath##note:x[<<1 Kgs 17:9–10; Luke 4:26>>]; and the captivity of Jerusalem, ##which __is__ in Sepharad, shall possess##note:x[<<Jer 32:44>>] the cities of the south.note:tn[__which…__: or, shall possess that which is in]
@ And ##saviours##note:x[<<Jas 5:20>>] shall come up on mount Zion ##to judge##note:x[<<Dan 7:27>>] the mount of Esau; ##and the##note:x[<<Zech 14:9>>] kingdom shall be the LORD’s.
