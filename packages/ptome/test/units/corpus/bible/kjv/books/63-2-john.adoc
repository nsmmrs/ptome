== @2JN

[.book-title]
The Second Epistle of John

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== A Greeting from the Elder

[.parallel]
(<<3 John 1:1–4>>)

@ The elder unto the elect lady and her children, ##whom##note:x[<<1 John 3:18>>] I love in the truth; and not I only, but also all they that have ##known##note:x[<<John 8:32; 1 Tim 2:4>>] the truth;
@ For the truth’s sake, ##which##note:x[<<John 15:7; 1 John 2:14, 17>>] dwelleth in us, and shall be with us for ever.
@ ##Grace##note:x[<<Rom 1:7; 1 Tim 1:2>>] be with you, mercy, __and__ peace, from God the Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ, ##the Son##note:x[<<1 John 4:10>>] of the Father, in truth and love.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Walking in the Truth

[.parallel]
(<<John 8:30–47>>)

@ I ##rejoiced##note:x[<<3 John 1:3–4>>] greatly that I found of thy children ##walking##note:x[<<Eph 5:2, 8>>] in truth, as we have received a commandment from the Father.
@ And now I beseech thee, lady, ##not##note:x[<<1 John 3:11>>] as though I wrote a new commandment unto thee, but that which we had from the beginning, ##that we##note:x[<<John 13:34–35; 15:12>>] love one another.
@ And ##this is love##note:x[<<John 15:10; 1 John 5:3>>], that we walk after his commandments. ##This is the##note:x[<<1 John 2:24>>] commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Beware of Deceivers

@ For ##many##note:x[<<2 Pet 2:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22; 4:1>>] deceivers are entered into the world, who confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh. This is a deceiver and an antichrist.
@ ##Look##note:x[<<Rev 3:11>>] to yourselves, ##that we lose##note:x[<<Heb 10:35; Rev 3:11>>] not those things which we have wrought, but that we receive a full reward.
@ Whosoever transgresseth, and ##abideth not##note:x[<<1 John 2:22–24>>] in ##the doctrine##note:x[<<John 7:16–17; Col 3:16>>] of Christ, hath not God. He that abideth in the doctrine of Christ, he hath both the Father and the Son.
@ If there ##come##note:x[<<Rom 16:17–18; 2 Tim 3:5–6; Titus 3:10>>] any unto you, and bring not this doctrine, receive him not into __your__ house, neither bid him God speed:
@ For he that biddeth him God speed is ##partaker##note:x[<<Eph 5:11; 1 Tim 5:22>>] of his evil deeds.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Conclusion

[.parallel]
(<<3 John 1:13–14>>)

@ Having ##many##note:x[<<John 16:12>>] things to write unto you, ##I would##note:x[<<3 John 1:13>>] not __write__ with paper and ink: but ##I trust##note:x[<<Rom 15:24>>] to come unto you, and speak face to face, that our joy may be full.
@ ##The children of thy elect sister greet thee. Amen.##note:x[<<1 Pet 5:13; 2 John 1:1>>]
