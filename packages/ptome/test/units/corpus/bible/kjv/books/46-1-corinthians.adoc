=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Greetings from Paul and Sosthenes

[.parallel]
(<<Acts 18:1–11; 2 Corinthians 1:1–2>>)

@ Paul, ##called##note:x[<<Rom 1:1>>] __to be__ an apostle of Jesus Christ ##through##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:1; Eph 1:1>>] the will of God, and Sosthenes __our__ brother,
@ Unto the church of God which is at Corinth, to them that are ##sanctified##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:30; 6:9–11>>] in Christ Jesus, ##called##note:x[<<Rom 1:7>>] __to be__ saints, with all that in every place call upon the name of Jesus Christ our Lord, both theirs and ours:
@ ##Grace __be__ unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and __from__ the Lord Jesus Christ.##note:x[<<Rom 1:7; 2 Cor 1:2; 1 Pet 1:2>>]

[discrete.pericope]
==== Thanksgiving

[.parallel]
(<<Philippians 1:3–11; Colossians 1:3–14>>)

@ I ##thank##note:x[<<Rom 1:8; 6:17>>] my God always on your behalf, for ##the grace##note:x[<<John 14:26>>] of God which is given you by Jesus Christ;
@ That ##in every##note:x[<<2 Cor 9:11>>] thing ye are enriched by him, ##in all##note:x[<<2 Cor 8:7>>] utterance, ##and __in__##note:x[<<Col 2:3>>] all knowledge;
@ Even as ##the##note:x[<<1 Tim 2:6; 2 Tim 1:8; Rev 1:2>>] testimony of Christ was confirmed in you:
@ So that ye come behind in no gift; ##waiting##note:x[<<Phil 3:20; Heb 9:28; 2 Pet 3:12>>] for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ:
@ Who shall also ##confirm##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:13>>] you unto the end, __that ye may be__ ##blameless##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:13; 5:23–24>>] in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.
@ ##God##note:x[<<Deut 7:9; 2 Thess 3:3; Heb 10:23>>] __is__ faithful, by whom ye were called unto the fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Unity in the Church

[.parallel]
(<<Psalms 133:1–3; Ephesians 4:1–16>>)

@ Now ##I beseech##note:x[<<Eph 4:1>>] you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, ##that ye##note:x[<<Eph 4:1–7; Phil 2:1–4>>] all speak the same thing, and __that__ there be no divisions among you; but __that__ ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.
@ For it hath been declared unto me of you, my brethren, by them __which are of the house__ of Chloe, that there are contentions among you.
@ Now this I say, that every one of you saith, ##I am##note:x[<<1 Cor 3:4–6, 21–23>>] of Paul; and I of Apollos; and I of ##Cephas##note:x[<<John 1:42>>]; and I of Christ.
@ Is ##Christ##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:4; Eph 4:5>>] divided? was Paul crucified for you? ##or##note:x[<<Acts 2:38>>] were ye baptized in the name of Paul?
@ I thank God that I baptized none of you, but ##Crispus##note:x[<<Acts 18:8>>] and ##Gaius##note:x[<<Rom 16:23>>];
@ Lest any should say that I had baptized in mine own name.
@ And I baptized also the ##household##note:x[<<1 Cor 16:15, 17>>] of Stephanas: besides, I know not whether I baptized any other.
@ For Christ sent me ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:1, 13; 2 Pet 1:16>>] to baptize, but to preach the gospel: not with wisdom of words, lest the cross of Christ should be made of none effect.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Message of the Cross

@ For the preaching of the cross is ##to##note:x[<<2 Thess 2:10>>] them that perish ##foolishness##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:14>>]; but ##unto##note:x[<<Rom 1:16>>] us which are saved it is the power of God.
@ ##For it is written, I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and will bring to nothing the understanding of the prudent.##note:x[<<Isa 29:14; Jer 8:9; 1 Cor 3:19>>]
@ Where __is__ the wise? where __is__ the scribe? where __is__ the disputer of this world? ##hath##note:x[<<Job 12:17; Rom 1:22; 1 Cor 1:19>>] not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?
@ For after that in the wisdom of God ##the world##note:x[<<Matt 11:25; Luke 10:21; Rom 1:20–22>>] by wisdom knew not God, it pleased God by the foolishness of preaching to save them that believe.
@ For ##the Jews##note:x[<<Matt 12:38–39; 16:1–4; Mark 8:11>>] require a sign, and the Greeks seek after wisdom:
@ But ##we##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:2>>] preach Christ crucified, ##unto the Jews##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:8>>] a stumblingblock, and unto the Greeks ##foolishness##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:14>>];
@ But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ ##the power##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:18>>] of God, and ##the wisdom##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:30; Col 2:3>>] of God.
@ Because ##the foolishness##note:x[<<Zech 4:6–7; Rom 11:33–36; 1 Cor 1:18>>] of God is wiser than men; and the weakness of God is stronger than men.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Wisdom from God

@ For ye see your calling, brethren, how ##that##note:x[<<1 Cor 3:18–20>>] not many wise men after the flesh, ##not many mighty##note:x[<<Jas 1:9–11; 2:5>>], not many noble, __are called__:
@ ##But God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise; and God hath chosen the weak things of the world to confound the things which are mighty;##note:x[<<Ps 8:2; Matt 11:25; Luke 21:15>>]
@ And base things of the world, and ##things which##note:x[<<Rom 4:17>>] are despised, hath God chosen, __yea__, and things which are not, ##to bring##note:x[<<Job 34:24; Isa 2:11>>] to nought things that are:
@ ##That no flesh should glory in his presence.##note:x[<<Jer 9:23; Rom 3:27; Eph 2:9>>]
@ But of him are ye ##in##note:x[<<Rom 8:1>>] Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, and ##righteousness##note:x[<<Phil 3:9>>], and sanctification, and ##redemption##note:x[<<Eph 1:7>>]:
@ ##That, according as it is written, He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord.##note:x[<<Jer 9:23–24; 2 Cor 10:17; Phil 3:3>>]

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Message by the Spirit’s Power

@ And I, brethren, when I came to you, came not ##with##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17; 2:4, 13>>] excellency of speech or of wisdom, declaring unto you the testimony of God.
@ For I determined ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:22–25; Gal 6:14; Phil 3:8–10>>] to know any thing among you, save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.
@ ##And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:10–13; 2 Cor 12:5–10; 13:4>>]
@ And my speech and my preaching __was__ ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17>>] with enticing words of man’s wisdom, ##but##note:x[<<1 Cor 4:20; 1 Thess 1:5>>] in demonstration of the Spirit and of power:
@ That your faith should not stand in the wisdom of men, ##but##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17; 2 Cor 4:7; 6:7>>] in the power of God.

@ But as it is written, ##Eye##note:x[<<Isa 64:4>>] hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, ##the things##note:x[<<Ps 31:19>>] which God hath prepared for ##them##note:x[<<Jas 1:12>>] that love him.

@ For ##what##note:x[<<Prov 14:10; 20:27>>] man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? ##even##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:10>>] so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God.

@ note:x[<<1 Cor 2:14>>]Which things also we speak, ##not##note:x[<<1 Cor 1:17; 2:4>>] in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.

@ For ##who##note:x[<<Isa 40:13–14; Rom 11:34>>] hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him? ##But##note:x[<<John 16:13–16>>] we have the mind of Christ.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Servants of Christ

@ But with me ##it is##note:x[<<John 7:24; 1 Cor 2:15>>] a very small thing that I should be judged of you, or of man’s judgment: yea, I judge not mine own self.
@ For ##I know##note:x[<<1 John 3:20–21>>] nothing by myself; ##yet##note:x[<<Ps 130:3; 143:2>>] am I not hereby justified: but he that judgeth me is the Lord.
@ Therefore judge nothing before the time, ##until##note:x[<<Jas 5:7>>] the Lord come, ##who##note:x[<<1 Cor 3:13>>] both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts: and then shall every man have ##praise##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:10>>] of God.
