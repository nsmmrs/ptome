The Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Galatians

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Greeting to the Galatians

@ Paul, ##an##note:x[<<Rom 1:1; 1 Cor 1:1>>] apostle, ( ##not##note:x[<<Gal 1:11–12>>] of men, neither by man, but by Jesus Christ, and God the Father, who raised him from the dead;)
@ And ##all##note:x[<<Phil 4:21>>] the brethren which are with me, unto the ##churches##note:x[<<Acts 16:5–6; 1 Cor 16:1>>] of Galatia:
@ ##Grace __be__ to you and peace from God the Father, and __from__ our Lord Jesus Christ,##note:x[<<Rom 1:7–15; 2 Cor 13:14; 2 John 1:3>>]
@ Who ##gave##note:x[<<Matt 20:28; Rom 4:25; Gal 2:20>>] himself for our sins, that he might deliver us from this present evil world, according to the will of God and our Father:
@ To ##whom##note:x[<<Rom 11:36; Jude 1:25>>] __be__ glory for ever and ever. Amen.

[discrete.pericope]
==== No Other Gospel

@ I marvel that ye are ##so##note:x[<<Isa 29:13; Gal 5:7>>] soon removed from him that called you into the grace of Christ ##unto##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:4>>] another gospel:
@ Which is not another; ##but##note:x[<<Acts 15:24; Rom 16:17–18>>] there be some that trouble you, and would ##pervert##note:x[<<Acts 15:24>>] the gospel of Christ.
@ But ##though##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:13–14; Gal 1:9; Rev 22:18–19>>] we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed.
@ As we said before, so say I now again, If any __man__ preach any other gospel unto you ##than##note:x[<<Deut 4:2; Prov 30:6; Rev 22:18–19>>] that ye have received, let him be accursed.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul Preaches the Gospel

@ For ##do I now##note:x[<<Acts 5:29; 1 Thess 2:4>>] persuade men, or God? or ##do I seek##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:4>>] to please men? for if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ.
@ But I certify you, brethren, ##that##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:9–10; Gal 1:1; Eph 3:3–8>>] the gospel which was preached of me is not after man.
@ For I neither received it of man, neither was I taught __it__, but by the revelation of Jesus Christ.
@ For ##ye##note:x[<<Acts 22:3–5>>] have heard of my conversation in time past in the Jews’ religion, ##how##note:x[<<Acts 8:3; 1 Cor 15:9>>] that beyond measure I persecuted the church of God, and wasted it:
@ And profited in the Jews’ religion above many my equals in mine own nation, ##being##note:x[<<Acts 22:3; 26:5>>] more exceedingly zealous of the ##traditions##note:x[<<Col 2:8>>] of my fathers.
@ But when it pleased God, ##who##note:x[<<Isa 49:1, 5; Jer 1:5>>] separated me from my mother’s womb, and called __me__ by his grace,
@ To ##reveal##note:x[<<Matt 16:17; Eph 3:5–10>>] his Son in me, that I might preach him among the heathen; immediately I conferred not with ##flesh##note:x[<<Matt 16:17>>] and blood:
@ Neither ##went##note:x[<<Acts 9:20–25>>] I up to Jerusalem to them which were apostles before me; but I went into Arabia, and returned again unto Damascus.
@ Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to see Peter, and abode with him fifteen days.
@ note:x[<<Matt 13:55; Mark 6:3>>]But other of the apostles saw I none, save James the Lord’s brother.
@ Now the things which I write unto you, ##behold##note:x[<<Rom 9:1; 2 Cor 11:31>>], before God, I lie not.
@ Afterwards ##I came##note:x[<<Acts 9:30; 15:41>>] into the regions of Syria and ##Cilicia##note:x[<<Acts 6:9>>];
@ And was unknown by face unto ##the churches##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:14>>] of Judaea which were in Christ:
@ But they had heard only, That ##he which##note:x[<<Acts 9:13; 1 Cor 15:8–10; 1 Tim 1:13–16>>] persecuted us in times past now preacheth the faith which once he destroyed.
@ ##And they glorified God in me.##note:x[<<Luke 15:10; Acts 11:18>>]

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Council at Jerusalem

[.parallel]
(<<Acts 15:5–21>>)

@ Then fourteen years after I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, and took Titus with __me__ also.
@ And I went up by revelation, and communicated unto them that gospel ##which##note:x[<<Gal 2:6>>] I preach among the Gentiles, but privately to them which were of reputation, lest by any means ##I should##note:x[<<Phil 2:16; 1 Thess 3:5>>] run, or had run, in vain.
@ ##But neither Titus, who was with me, being a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised:##note:x[<<Acts 16:3; 1 Cor 9:20–21; Gal 5:2–6>>]
@ And that ##because##note:x[<<1 John 4:1>>] of false brethren ##unawares##note:x[<<Jude 1:4>>] brought in, who came in privily to spy out our ##liberty##note:x[<<Gal 5:1>>] which we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into bondage:
@ To whom we gave place by subjection, no, not for an hour; ##that##note:x[<<Gal 2:14; 4:16>>] the truth of the gospel might continue with you.
@ But of ##these who##note:x[<<Gal 2:2>>] seemed to be somewhat, ( whatsoever they were, it maketh no matter to me: ##God##note:x[<<Acts 10:34; Rom 2:11>>] accepteth no man’s person:) for they who seemed __to be somewhat__ in conference added nothing to me:
@ But contrariwise, when they saw that ##the gospel of the uncircumcision##note:x[<<Gal 1:16; 1 Thess 2:4; 1 Tim 2:7>>] was committed unto me, as __the gospel__ of the circumcision __was__ unto Peter;
@ ( For he that wrought effectually in Peter to the apostleship of the circumcision, ##the same##note:x[<<Acts 9:15; 22:21>>] was mighty in me toward the Gentiles:)
@ And when James, Cephas, and John, who seemed to be ##pillars##note:x[<<Rev 3:12>>], perceived ##the grace##note:x[<<Rom 12:3; Eph 3:8>>] that was given unto me, they gave to me and Barnabas the right hands of fellowship; that we __should go__ unto the heathen, and they unto the circumcision.
@ Only __they would__ ##that##note:x[<<Acts 24:17; Heb 13:16; 1 John 3:17>>] we should remember the poor; the same which I also was forward to do.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul Confronts Cephas

@ But when Peter was come to Antioch, ##I withstood##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:16; Gal 2:5; 1 Tim 5:20>>] him to the face, because he was to be blamed.
@ For before that certain came from James, ##he did##note:x[<<Acts 10:28; Eph 3:6>>] eat with the Gentiles: but when they were come, ##he withdrew##note:x[<<Luke 15:2>>] and separated himself, fearing them which were of the circumcision.
@ And ##the other##note:x[<<1 Cor 15:33>>] Jews dissembled likewise with him; insomuch that Barnabas also was ##carried##note:x[<<Eph 4:14; Heb 13:9>>] away with their dissimulation.
@ But when I saw that they walked not uprightly according to ##the truth##note:x[<<Gal 2:5>>] of the gospel, I said unto Peter before __them__ all, ##If thou##note:x[<<Acts 10:28>>], being a Jew, livest after the manner of Gentiles, and not as do the Jews, ##why##note:x[<<Acts 15:10–11>>] compellest thou the Gentiles to live as do the Jews?
@ We __who are__ ##Jews##note:x[<<Eph 2:3>>] by nature, and not ##sinners##note:x[<<Rom 3:9; Titus 3:3>>] of the Gentiles,
@ Knowing ##that##note:x[<<Phil 3:9>>] a man is not justified by the works of the law, ##but##note:x[<<Phil 3:9>>] by the faith of Jesus Christ, even we have believed in Jesus Christ, that we might be justified by the faith of Christ, and not by the works of the law: ##for##note:x[<<Ps 143:2>>] by the works of the law shall no flesh be justified.
@ But if, while we seek to be justified by Christ, we ourselves also ##are found##note:x[<<Rom 6:1–2; 1 John 3:8–10>>] sinners, ##__is__##note:x[<<1 John 3:5>>] therefore Christ the minister of sin? God forbid.
@ ##For if I build again the things which I destroyed, I make myself a transgressor.##note:x[<<Gal 2:4–5; 4:9–12>>]
@ For I through the law am ##dead##note:x[<<Rom 6:2; 7:4>>] to the law, ##that##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:10>>] I might live unto God.
@ I am ##crucified##note:x[<<Rom 6:4–6; Gal 5:24>>] with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which ##I now##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:15>>] live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.
@ I do not frustrate the grace of God: for if ##righteousness##note:x[<<Rom 11:6; Gal 3:21; Heb 7:11>>] __come__ by the law, then Christ is dead in vain.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Faith and Belief

[.parallel]
(<<James 2:14–26>>)

@ O foolish Galatians, ##who##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:3; Gal 5:7–8>>] hath bewitched you, that ##ye##note:x[<<Gal 5:7>>] should not obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth, crucified among you?
@ This only would I learn of you, ##Received##note:x[<<Acts 15:8; Gal 3:14>>] ye the Spirit by the works of the law, or ##by the hearing##note:x[<<Rom 10:16–17>>] of faith?
@ Are ye so foolish? ##having##note:x[<<Gal 4:7–10; 5:4–8; Heb 7:16–19>>] begun in the Spirit, are ye now made perfect by the flesh?
@ Have ##ye##note:x[<<Heb 10:32–39; 2 John 1:8>>] suffered so many things in vain? if __it be__ yet in vain.
@ He therefore ##that##note:x[<<Gal 3:2>>] ministereth to you the Spirit, and ##worketh##note:x[<<Acts 14:9–10; 19:11–12>>] miracles among you, __doeth he it__ by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?
@ Even ##as##note:x[<<Gen 15:6; Rom 4:21–22; Jas 2:23>>] Abraham believed God, and it was accounted to him for righteousness.

@ ##Know##note:x[<<Ps 100:3>>] ye therefore that ##they##note:x[<<Rom 9:7–8; Gal 3:26–29>>] which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham.

@ ##So then they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham.##note:x[<<Rom 4:16; Gal 3:29; 4:28>>]

[discrete.pericope]
==== Christ Has Redeemed Us

@ For ##as many##note:x[<<Rom 8:7>>] as are of the works of the law are under the curse: for it is written, ##Cursed##note:x[<<Deut 27:26; Rom 3:19–20>>] __is__ every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them.
@ But that no man is justified by the law in the sight of God, __it is__ evident: for, ##The just##note:x[<<Hab 2:4; Rom 1:17; Heb 10:38>>] shall live by faith.
@ And ##the law##note:x[<<Rom 10:5–6>>] is not of faith: but, ##The man##note:x[<<Lev 18:5; Rom 10:5–6>>] that doeth them shall live in them.
@ Christ hath ##redeemed##note:x[<<1 Pet 2:24>>] us from the curse of the law, being made a curse ##for##note:x[<<Deut 21:23; 1 Pet 2:24>>] us: for it is written, Cursed __is__ every one that hangeth on a tree:
@ That the blessing of Abraham ##might##note:x[<<1 Cor 12:13; Gal 3:2; Eph 2:18>>] come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

[discrete.pericope]
==== The Purpose of the Law

[.parallel]
(<<Romans 7:1–6>>)

@ Brethren, ##I speak##note:x[<<Rom 6:19>>] after the manner of men; Though ##__it be__##note:x[<<Heb 9:17>>] but a man’s covenant, yet __if it be__ confirmed, no man disannulleth, or addeth thereto.
@ Now ##to##note:x[<<Gen 17:7–8; 22:17–18>>] Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, ##which##note:x[<<Gal 3:27–29>>] is Christ.
@ And this I say, __that__ ##the covenant##note:x[<<Heb 11:39–40>>], that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, ##which##note:x[<<Gen 15:13; Exod 12:40–41>>] was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul, that it should make the promise of none effect.
@ For ##if##note:x[<<Rom 4:13–16; Gal 2:21>>] the inheritance __be__ of the law, __it is__ no more of promise: ##but##note:x[<<Heb 6:12–15>>] God gave __it__ to Abraham by promise.
@ Wherefore ##then##note:x[<<Rom 7:7–13>>] __serveth__ the law? ##It was added##note:x[<<Rom 7:7–13>>] because of transgressions, till the seed should come to whom the promise was made; __and it was__ ordained ##by##note:x[<<Acts 7:53>>] angels in the hand of a mediator.
@ Now ##a mediator is##note:x[<<Job 9:33; 1 Tim 2:5>>] not __a mediator__ of one, ##but##note:x[<<Deut 6:4>>] God is one.
@ __Is__ the law then against the promises of God? God forbid: ##for##note:x[<<Rom 3:20; Gal 2:21>>] if there had been a law given which could have given life, verily ##righteousness##note:x[<<Rom 9:31>>] should have been by the law.
@ But the scripture hath ##concluded##note:x[<<Rom 11:32>>] all under sin, ##that##note:x[<<Heb 9:15>>] the promise by faith of Jesus Christ might be given ##to##note:x[<<John 11:25–26>>] them that believe.

@ But before ##faith came##note:x[<<Gal 3:19, 24–25>>], we were kept ##under##note:x[<<Gal 5:18>>] the law, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed.
@ Wherefore ##the law##note:x[<<Acts 13:38–39; Rom 10:4; Heb 10:1–14>>] was our schoolmaster __to bring us__ unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith.
@ But after that faith is come, ##we##note:x[<<Rom 6:14; 7:4; Heb 10:15–18>>] are no longer under a schoolmaster.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Sons and Heirs

@ Now I say, __That__ the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;
@ But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.
@ Even so we, when we were children, were ##in##note:x[<<Gal 4:9>>] bondage under the ##elements##note:x[<<Col 2:8, 20>>] of the world:
@ But when ##the fulness##note:x[<<Mark 1:15>>] of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, ##made##note:x[<<John 1:14>>] ##of a##note:x[<<Isa 7:14>>] woman, made under the law,
@ To ##redeem##note:x[<<Heb 9:15; 1 Pet 1:18–20>>] them that were under the law, ##that we##note:x[<<Eph 1:5>>] might receive the adoption of sons.
@ And because ye are sons, ##God##note:x[<<Rom 5:5; 8:15–17>>] hath sent forth ##the Spirit##note:x[<<Rom 8:15>>] of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.
@ Wherefore thou art no more a servant, ##but##note:x[<<Gal 3:26>>] a son; and ##if##note:x[<<Rom 8:16–17; Gal 3:29>>] a son, then an heir of God through Christ.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Paul’s Concern for the Galatians

@ Howbeit then, ##when##note:x[<<Eph 2:11–12; 1 Thess 4:5>>] ye knew not God, ##ye did##note:x[<<1 Cor 8:4>>] service unto them which by nature are no gods.
@ But now, after that ye have known God, or rather ##are known##note:x[<<1 Cor 8:3>>] of God, ##how##note:x[<<Rom 8:3; Gal 3:3>>] turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements, whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?
@ ##Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years.##note:x[<<Rom 14:5; Col 2:16–17>>]
@ I am afraid of you, ##lest##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:5>>] I have bestowed upon you labour in vain.

@ Brethren, I beseech you, ##be##note:x[<<1 Kgs 22:4>>] as I __am__; for I __am__ as ##ye##note:x[<<2 Cor 2:5>>] __are__: ye have not injured me at all.
@ Ye know how ##through##note:x[<<1 Cor 2:3; 2 Cor 10:10; 12:7–10>>] infirmity of the flesh I preached the gospel unto you at the first.
@ And my temptation which was in my flesh ye despised not, nor rejected; but received me as ##an angel##note:x[<<Heb 13:2>>] of God, __even__ ##as Christ##note:x[<<Matt 10:40; 1 Thess 2:13>>] Jesus.
@ Where is then ##the blessedness##note:x[<<Rom 5:2>>] ye spake of? for I bear you record, that, if __it had been__ possible, ye would have plucked out your own eyes, and have given them to me.
@ Am I therefore ##become##note:x[<<Prov 9:8; John 8:45>>] your enemy, ##because##note:x[<<Gal 5:7>>] I tell you the truth?
@ They ##zealously##note:x[<<Rom 16:18; 2 Cor 11:13–15; 2 Pet 2:3>>] affect you, __but__ not well; yea, they would exclude you, that ye might affect them.
@ But ##__it is__##note:x[<<Rev 3:19>>] good to be zealously affected always in __a__ good __thing__, and not only when ##I am##note:x[<<Phil 1:27; 2:12>>] present with you.
@ My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until ##Christ##note:x[<<Rom 8:29; 13:14; Eph 4:24>>] be formed in you,
@ I desire to be present with you now, and to change my voice; for I stand in doubt of you.

@ Behold, I Paul say unto you, ##that##note:x[<<Acts 15:1; Gal 2:3–5; 5:6>>] if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.

@ ##Bear##note:x[<<Rom 15:1; 1 Thess 5:14>>] ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil ##the law##note:x[<<John 13:34>>] of Christ.
@ For ##if##note:x[<<Prov 26:12; 1 Cor 3:18; 8:2>>] a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.
@ But let every man ##prove##note:x[<<Ps 26:2; 1 Cor 11:28; 2 Cor 13:5>>] his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.
@ ##For every man shall bear his own burden.##note:x[<<Jer 32:19; Rom 14:10–12; 2 Cor 5:10–11>>]
@ ##Let him that is taught in the word communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things.##note:x[<<Deut 12:19; Rom 15:27; 1 Tim 5:17–18>>]
@ Be not deceived; God is not mocked: ##for##note:x[<<Job 4:8; Hos 10:12; 2 Cor 9:6>>] whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.
@ For he that ##soweth to his##note:x[<<Rom 8:13; Jas 3:18>>] flesh shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that ##soweth to the##note:x[<<Jas 3:18>>] Spirit shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.
@ And let ##us##note:x[<<1 Cor 15:58; 2 Thess 3:13>>] not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, ##if##note:x[<<Isa 40:30–31>>] we faint not.
@ As we have therefore opportunity, let us ##do good##note:x[<<1 Thess 5:15; Titus 3:8; Heb 13:16>>] unto all __men__, especially unto them who are of the household of faith.

==== Final Warnings and Blessings

@ Ye see how large a letter I have ##written##note:x[<<1 Cor 16:21–23>>] unto you with mine own hand.
@ As many ##as desire##note:x[<<2 Cor 11:13>>] to make a fair shew in the flesh, ##they constrain##note:x[<<Acts 15:1>>] you to be circumcised; only ##lest##note:x[<<Gal 5:11>>] they should suffer persecution for the cross of Christ.
@ For neither they themselves who are circumcised ##keep##note:x[<<Rom 2:17–24; 2 Pet 2:19>>] the law; but desire to have you circumcised, ##that they may##note:x[<<1 Cor 5:6>>] glory in your flesh.
@ But God forbid ##that I##note:x[<<Jer 9:23–24>>] should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom ##the world##note:x[<<Rom 6:6; Gal 2:20>>] is crucified unto me, and I unto the world.
@ For ##in##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:17>>] Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision, ##but##note:x[<<2 Cor 5:17; Col 3:10–11>>] a new creature.
@ And as many as walk according to this rule, peace __be__ on them, and mercy, and upon ##the Israel##note:x[<<Rom 9:6–8; Gal 3:29; Phil 3:3>>] of God.
@ From henceforth let no man trouble me: for ##I bear##note:x[<<2 Cor 1:5; 11:23–25; Col 1:24>>] in my body the marks of the Lord Jesus.
@ Brethren, ##the grace##note:x[<<Rom 16:20; 2 Cor 13:14; 2 Tim 4:22>>] of our Lord Jesus Christ __be__ with your spirit. Amen.
