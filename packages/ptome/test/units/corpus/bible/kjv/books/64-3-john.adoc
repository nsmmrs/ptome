== @3JN

[.book-title]
The Third Epistle of John

=== @1

[discrete.pericope]
==== A Greeting from the Elder

[.parallel]
(<<2 John 1:1–3>>)

@ The elder unto ##the wellbeloved##note:x[<<Rom 16:23; 1 Cor 1:14>>] Gaius, ##whom##note:x[<<1 John 3:18>>] I love in the truth.

@ Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, ##even##note:x[<<Col 1:4–6; 2 Pet 1:3–9; 3:18>>] as thy soul prospereth.
@ For ##I##note:x[<<2 John 1:4>>] rejoiced greatly, ##when##note:x[<<1 Thess 3:6–9>>] the brethren came and testified of ##the truth##note:x[<<2 John 1:4>>] that is in thee, even as thou walkest in the truth.
@ I ##have##note:x[<<Prov 23:24>>] no greater joy than to hear ##that##note:x[<<Gal 4:19>>] my children ##walk##note:x[<<2 Kgs 20:3>>] in truth.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Gaius Commended for Hospitality

@ ##Beloved, thou doest faithfully whatsoever thou doest to the brethren, and to strangers;##note:x[<<Luke 12:42; 16:10–12; Col 3:17>>]
@ Which have borne witness of thy charity before the church: ##whom##note:x[<<Acts 15:3; Titus 3:13>>] if thou bring forward on their journey ##after a godly sort##note:x[<<1 Thess 2:12>>], thou shalt do well:
@ Because ##that##note:x[<<2 Cor 4:5; Rev 2:3>>] for his name’s sake they went forth, ##taking##note:x[<<2 Kgs 5:15–16>>] nothing of the Gentiles.
@ We therefore ought ##to receive##note:x[<<2 Cor 7:2–3>>] such, that we might be ##fellowhelpers##note:x[<<1 Cor 3:5–9; 16:10–11>>] to the truth.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Diotrephes and Demetrius

@ I wrote unto the church: but Diotrephes, ##who loveth##note:x[<<Matt 23:4–8; Luke 22:24–27; Titus 1:7–16>>] to have the preeminence among them, receiveth us not.
@ Wherefore, if I come, ##I will##note:x[<<2 Cor 13:2>>] remember his deeds which he doeth, ##prating##note:x[<<Prov 10:8>>] against us with malicious words: and not content therewith, neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and forbiddeth them that would, ##and casteth##note:x[<<Isa 66:5>>] __them__ out of the church.

@ Beloved, ##follow##note:x[<<Ps 37:27; Isa 1:16–17; Eph 5:1>>] not that which is evil, but that which is good. He that doeth good is of God: but he that doeth evil hath not seen God.
@ Demetrius hath ##good##note:x[<<Acts 10:22; 1 Tim 3:7>>] report of all __men__, and of the truth itself: yea, ##and we##note:x[<<John 21:24>>] __also__ bear record; and ye know that our record is true.

[discrete.pericope]
==== Conclusion

[.parallel]
(<<2 John 1:12–13>>)

@ ##I had many things to write, but I will not with ink and pen write unto thee:##note:x[<<2 John 1:12>>]
@ But I trust I shall shortly see thee, and we shall speak face to face. Peace __be__ to thee. __Our__ friends salute thee. Greet the friends by name.
