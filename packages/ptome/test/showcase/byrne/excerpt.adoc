=== Proposition I. Problem.

image::prop-1.svg["Two equal circles, blue and red, each through the other’s centre; the black line joins the centres, and the yellow and red lines join its ends to a point where the circles cross",float=right]

[.enunciation.dropcap]
On a given finite straight line ({black}) to describe an equilateral triangle.

Describe image:prop-1-circle-blue.svg[the blue circle] and image:prop-1-circle-red.svg[the red circle] (postulate 3.); draw {yellow} and {red} (post. 1.).

then will image:prop-1-triangle.svg[the triangle] be equilateral.

[.steps%hardbreaks]
For {black} {eq} {yellow} (def. 15.);
and {black} {eq} {red} (def. 15.),
{therefore} {yellow} {eq} {red} (axiom. 1.);
and therefore image:prop-1-triangle.svg[the triangle] is the equilateral triangle required.

[.qed]
Q.{nbsp}E.{nbsp}D.
