# The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire: Volume I

Edward Gibbon's first volume (Chapters I–XVI, with the prefaces and the
introduction of the edition Standard Ebooks follows), in a look after
the early editions Strahan and Cadell printed. What the work shows:
heavy footnotes — 1,931 of them, Gibbon's and his editors' (Milman,
Guizot, Wenck, Smith) — set at the foot in two balanced columns, kept on
their references' pages, a long note going on at the foot of the next
page (`footnotes_columns`, FEAT-6nsldy); the dates in the outer margin
as side notes; the chapters' summaries; the running head across the
spread.

Imported by `tool/showcase_import.dart` with Chapters XVII–LXXI, the
appendix and the preface to the fourth volume left out (`--skip`), then
edited by hand:

- The chapters headed "Chap. I.", as the early editions head them.
- The marginal dates, which Standard Ebooks writes as ISO dates
  (`AD 268-03-20`), as the editions set them: "A.D. 268. March 20."
- `gibbon.adoc`: the volume's title, the book set for print
  (`:media: prepress`), so that the dates go in the outer margin.

The notes refer to one another and to chapters of the later volumes:
those references are their text, the links left out.

## Where it departs from them

- The text is Standard Ebooks' (the Milman edition's, with the editors'
  notes among Gibbon's, marked with their initials); the octavo's long s
  and catchwords are not set.
- The octavo sets its notes at the end of each chapter, the quarto at
  the foot in two columns: here they are at the foot, in two columns.
- The octavo's glosses in the margin summarize the text ("Encampment.");
  Standard Ebooks keeps only the dates, which are set there.
