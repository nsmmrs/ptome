# College Physics 2e: Kinematics

Chapter 2, "Kinematics", of OpenStax's College Physics 2e, in a textbook
design of the work's own. What the work shows: a modern textbook's parts
— each section's learning objectives in a box, its notes ("Misconception
Alert", "Making Connections") in boxes, its worked examples numbered by
chapter in ruled boxes, set out in their steps (strategy, solution,
discussion), its checks of understanding with their solutions; the
equations, displayed and in the text, typeset from AsciiMath (MathML in
the EPUB); the figures numbered by chapter with their descriptions as
alternative text; the sections' summaries, conceptual questions and
problems collected at the chapter's end, the problems numbered through
the chapter, their answers in an answer key at the back, each linked to
its problem; the chapter's glossary (FEAT-3gkhqx). The equations found
BUG-rvh0f1: a character the math font lacks was drawn as its .notdef.

No reference scan is set beside the pages: the look is the work's own,
not the publisher's.
