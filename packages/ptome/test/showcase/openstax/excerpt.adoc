[#m42033]
=== 2.1 Displacement

[.learning-objectives]
.Learning Objectives
****

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

* Define position, displacement, distance, and distance traveled.
* Explain the relationship between position and displacement.
* Distinguish between displacement and distance traveled.
* Calculate displacement and distance given initial position, final position, and the path between the two.

****

[#fs-id3206000.note]
.Displacement
****

Displacement is the __change in position__ of an object:

[stem#eip-458]
++++
Delta x = x_("f") - x_(0) ,
++++

where stem:[Delta x] is displacement, stem:[x_("f")] is the final position, and stem:[x_(0)] is the initial position.

****

[#fs-id1744930]
.Calculating Displacement: A Subway Train
====

What are the magnitude and sign of displacements for the motions of the subway train shown in parts (a) and (b) of the figure?

[.step]
Strategy

A drawing with a coordinate system is already provided, so we don’t need to make a sketch, but we should analyze it to make sure we understand what it is showing. Pay particular attention to the coordinate system. To find displacement, we use the equation stem:[Delta x = x_(f) - x_(0)]. This is straightforward since the initial and final positions are given.

[.step]
Solution

{empty}1. Identify the knowns. In the figure we see that stem:[x_(f) = "6.70 km"] and stem:[x_(0) = "4.70 km"] for part (a), and stem:[{:x ':}_(f) = 3 ".75 km"] and stem:[{:x ':}_(0) = 5 ".25 km"] for part (b).

{empty}2. Solve for displacement in part (a).

[stem#import-auto-id2400875]
++++
Delta x = x_(f) - x_(0) = 6 "." "70 km" - 4 "." "70 km" "=" " " "+" 2 "." "00 km"
++++

{empty}3. Solve for displacement in part (b).

[stem#import-auto-id2589758]
++++
Delta x ' = {:x ':}_(f) - {:x ':}_(0) = "3.75 km" - "5.25 km" = - "1.50 km"
++++

[.step]
Discussion

The direction of the motion in (a) is to the right and therefore its displacement has a positive sign, whereas motion in (b) is to the left and thus has a negative sign.
