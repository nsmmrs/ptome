== Chap. X.

My uncle __Toby’s__ head at that time was full of other matters, so that it was not till the demolition of __Dunkirk__, when all the other civilities of __Europe__ were settled, that he found leisure to return this.

This made an armistice (that is, speaking with regard to my uncle __Toby__⁠—but with respect to Mrs. __Wadman__, a vacancy)⁠—of almost eleven years. But in all cases of this nature, as it is the second blow, happen at what distance of time it will, which makes the fray⁠⸺⁠I choose for that reason to call these the amours of my uncle __Toby__ with Mrs. __Wadman__, rather than the amours of Mrs. __Wadman__ with my uncle __Toby__.

This is not a distinction without a difference.

It is not like the affair of __an old hat cock’d__⁠⸺⁠and __a cock’d old hat__, about which your reverences have so often been at odds with one another⁠⸺⁠but there is a difference here in the nature of things⁠⸺⁠

And let me tell you, gentry, a wide one too.
