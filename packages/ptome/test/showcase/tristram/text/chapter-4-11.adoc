== Chap. XI.

We shall bring all things to rights, said my father, setting his foot upon the first step from the landing.⁠—This __Trismegistus__, continued my father, drawing his leg back and turning to my uncle __Toby__⁠⸺⁠was the greatest (__Toby__) of all earthly beings⁠—he was the greatest king⁠⸺⁠the greatest lawgiver⁠⸺⁠the greatest philosopher⁠⸺⁠and the greatest priest⁠⸺⁠and engineer⁠—said my uncle __Toby__.

⸻In course, said my father.
