== Chap. XXXVIII.

To conceive this right,⁠—call for pen and ink⁠—here’s paper ready to your hand.⁠⸺⁠Sit down, Sir, paint her to your own mind⁠⸺⁠as like your mistress as you can⁠⸺⁠as unlike your wife as your conscience will let you⁠—’tis all one to me⁠⸺⁠please but your own fancy in it.

⸻Was ever anything in Nature so sweet!⁠—so exquisite!

⸺⁠Then, dear Sir, how could my uncle __Toby__ resist it?

Thrice happy book! thou wilt have one page, at least, within thy covers, which [.sc]#Malice# will not blacken, and which [.sc]#Ignorance# cannot misrepresent.
