== Chap. XXVII.

My father put on his spectacles⁠—looked,⁠—took them off,⁠—put them into the case⁠—all in less than a statutable minute; and without opening his lips, turned about and walked precipitately downstairs: my mother imagined he had stepped down for lint and basilicon; but seeing him return with a couple of folios under his arm, and __Obadiah__ following him with a large reading-desk, she took it for granted ’twas an herbal, and so drew him a chair to the bedside, that he might consult upon the case at his ease.

⸺⁠If it be but right done,⁠—said my father, turning to the [lang=la]__Section⁠—de sede vel subjecto circumcisionis__,⁠⸺⁠for he had brought up [lang=la]__Spenser de Legibus Hebraeorum Ritualibus__⁠—and __Maimonides__, in order to confront and examine us altogether.⁠—

⸺⁠If it be but right done, quoth he:⁠—only tell us, cried my mother, interrupting him, what herbs?⁠⸺⁠For that, replied my father, you must send for Dr. __Slop__.

My mother went down, and my father went on, reading the section as follows,
