== Chap. XVI.

—If you have any objection,⁠—said my father, addressing himself to Dr. __Slop__. Not in the least, replied Dr. __Slop__;⁠—for it does not appear on which side of the question it is wrote;⁠⸺⁠it may be a composition of a divine of our church, as well as yours,⁠—so that we run equal risques.⁠⸺’Tis wrote upon neither side, quoth __Trim__, for ’tis only upon __Conscience__, an’ please your Honours.

__Trim’s__ reason put his audience into good-humour,⁠—all but Dr. __Slop__, who turning his head about towards __Trim__, looked a little angry.

Begin, __Trim__,⁠—and read distinctly, quoth my father.⁠—I will, an’ please your Honour, replied the Corporal, making a bow, and bespeaking attention with a slight movement of his right hand.
