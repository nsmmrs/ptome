[#chapter-9-16]
== Chap. XVI.

When my uncle __Toby__ and the corporal had marched down to the bottom of the avenue, they recollected their business lay the other way; so they faced about and marched up straight to Mrs. __Wadman’s__ door.

I warrant your honour; said the corporal, touching his __Montero__-cap with his hand, as he passed him in order to give a knock at the door⁠⸺⁠My uncle __Toby__, contrary to his invariable way of treating his faithful servant, said nothing good or bad: the truth was, he had not altogether marshal’d his ideas; he wish’d for another conference, and as the corporal was mounting up the three steps before the door⁠—he hem’d twice⁠—a portion of my uncle __Toby’s__ most modest spirits fled, at each expulsion, towards the corporal; he stood with the rapper of the door suspended for a full minute in his hand, he scarce knew why. __Bridget__ stood perdue within, with her finger and her thumb upon the latch, benumb’d with expectation; and Mrs. __Wadman__, with an eye ready to be deflowered again, sat breathless behind the window-curtain of her bedchamber, watching their approach.

__Trim!__ said my uncle __Toby__⁠⸺⁠but as he articulated the word, the minute expired, and __Trim__ let fall the rapper.

My uncle __Toby__ perceiving that all hopes of a conference were knock’d on the head by it⁠⸻whistled Lillabullero.
