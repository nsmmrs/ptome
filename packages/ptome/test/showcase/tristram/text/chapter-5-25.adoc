== Chap. XXV.

’Tis a point settled,⁠—and I mention it for the comfort of __Confucius__,footnote:[Mr. __Shandy__ is supposed to mean &#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;&#42; &#42;&#42;&#42; Esq.; member for &#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;&#42;,⁠⸺⁠and not the __Chinese__ Legislator.] who is apt to get entangled in telling a plain story⁠—that provided he keeps along the line of his story,⁠—he may go backwards and forwards as he will,⁠—’tis still held to be no digression.

This being premised, I take the benefit of the __act of going backwards__ myself.
