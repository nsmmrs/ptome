[#the-church]
== The Church

=== The Altar.

[.altar.altar-1]
A broken [.spaced-cap]##A##[.spaced]##LTAR##, Lord, thy servant reares;
Made of a heart, and cemented with teares:

[.altar.altar-2]
Whose parts are as thy hand did frame;
No workmans tool hath touch'd the same.

[.altar.altar-3]
A [.spaced-cap]##H##[.spaced]##EART## alone
Is such a stone,
As nothing but
Thy power doth cut.
Wherefore each part
Of my hard heart
Meets in this frame,
To praise thy name:

[.altar.altar-4]
That if I chance to hold my peace,
These stones to praise thee may not cease.

[.altar.altar-5]
O let thy blessed [.spaced-cap]##S##[.spaced]##ACRIFICE## be mine,
And sanctifie this [.spaced-cap]##A##[.spaced]##LTAR## to be thine.

=== The Church-porch.

[.v]
When thou dost tell anothers jest, therein
Omit the oathes, which true wit cannot need:
Pick out of tales the mirth, but not the sinne.
He pares his apple, that will cleanly feed.
[.i95]##{nbsp}##Play not away the vertue of that name,
[.i95]##{nbsp}##Which is thy best stake, when griefs make thee [.turn-up]##tame.##

[.v]
Flie idlenesse, which yet thou canst not flie
By dressing, mistressing, and complement.
If those take up thy day, the sunne will crie
Against thee: for his light was onely lent.
[.i95]##{nbsp}##God gave thy soul brave wings; put not those fea[.turn-up]##thers##
[.i95]##{nbsp}##Into a bed, to sleep out all ill weathers.
