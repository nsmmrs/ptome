== A Boat, Beneath a Sunny Sky

ifdef::backend-pdf[]
image::blank.svg["",role=cut,pdfwidth=1pt,pdfheight=85.9pt]

image::double-rule.svg["",role=cut,pdfwidth=61pt,align=center]

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-top=18.6pt,margin-bottom=0]
____
                A boat, beneath a sunny sky,
                Lingering onward dreamily
                In an evening of July⁠—
____

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-top=30.0pt,margin-bottom=0]
____
                Children three that nestle near,
                Eager eye and willing ear,
                Pleased a simple tale to hear⁠—
____

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-top=28.6pt,margin-bottom=0]
____
                Long has paled that sunny sky:
                Echoes fade and memories die:
                Autumn frosts have slain July.
____

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-top=25.0pt,margin-bottom=0]
____
                Still she haunts me, phantomwise,
                Alice moving under skies
                Never seen by waking eyes.
____

[#looking-glass-envoi-2.envoi%notitle]
== Children Yet, the Tale to Hear

ifdef::backend-pdf[]
image::blank.svg["",role=cut,pdfwidth=1pt,pdfheight=4.3pt]
endif::[]

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-bottom=0]
____
                Children yet, the tale to hear,
                Eager eye and willing ear,
                Lovingly shall nestle near.
____

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-top=29.1pt,margin-bottom=0]
____
                In a Wonderland they lie,
                Dreaming as the days go by,
                Dreaming as the summers die:
____

[verse.poem.conclusion.v10.lh157,margin-top=26.8pt,margin-bottom=0]
____
                Ever drifting down the stream⁠—
                Lingering in the golden gleam⁠—
                Life, what is it but a dream?
____

[.the-end,margin-top=72.0pt,margin-bottom=0]
THE END.
