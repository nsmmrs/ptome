[.braces]
* Cure of melancholy is either
** <<id_2.1.1,Sect 1.>> General to all, which contains
*** Unlawful means forbidden,
**** <<id_2.1.1,Memb. 1.>> From the devil, magicians, witches, &amp;c., by charms, spells, incantations, &amp;c.
***** __Quest. 1.__ Whether they can cure this, or other such like diseases?
***** __Quest. 2.__ Whether, if they can so cure, it be lawful to seek to them for help?
*** or Lawful means, which are
**** <<id_2.1.2,Memb. 2.>> Immediately from God, [.foreign,lang=la]##a Jove principium## by prayer &amp;c.
***** <<id_2.1.3,Memb. 3.>> __Quest. 1.__ Whether saints and their relics can help this infirmity?
***** __Quest. 2.__ Whether it be lawful to sue to them for aid.
**** or <<id_2.1.4,Memb. 4.>> Mediately by Nature which concerns and works by
***** <<id_2.1.4.1,Subsect. 1.>> Physician, in whom is required science, confidence, honesty, &amp;c.
***** <<id_2.1.4.2,Subsect. 2.>> Patient, in whom is required obedience, constancy, willingness, patience, confidence, bounty, &amp;c., not to practise on himself.
***** <<id_2.1.4.3,Subsect. 3.>> Physic, which consists of
- Dietetical <<aries.2,♈︎>>
- Pharmaceutical <<taurus.2,♉︎>>
- Chirurgical <<gemini.2,♊︎>>
** or Particular to the three distinct species, <<cancer.2,♋︎>> <<leo.2,♌︎>> <<virgo.2,♍︎>>

[.braces]
* [[aries.2]]♈︎ <<id_2.2.1,Sect. 2.>> Dietetical, which consists in reforming those six non-natural things, as in
** Diet rectified <<id_2.2.1,1. Memb.>>
*** Matter and quality <<id_2.2.1.1,1 Subs.>>
**** Such meats as are easy of digestion, well-dressed, hot, sod, &amp;c., young, moist, of good nourishment, &amp;c.
**** Bread of pure wheat, well-baked.
**** Water clear from the fountain.
**** Wine and drink not too strong, &amp;c.
**** Flesh
***** Mountain birds, partridge, pheasant, quails, &amp;c. Hen, capon, mutton, veal, kid, rabbit, &amp;c.
**** Fish
***** That live in gravelly waters, as pike, perch, trout, sea-fish, solid, white, &amp;c.
**** Herbs
***** Borage, bugloss, balm, succory, endive, violets, in broth, not raw, &amp;c.
**** Fruits and roots.
***** Raisins of the sun, apples corrected for wind, oranges, &amp;c., parsnips, potatoes, &amp;c.
*** or <<id_2.2.1.2,Subs. 2.>> Quantity.
**** At seasonable and unusual times of repast, in good order, not before the first be concocted, sparing, not overmuch of one dish.
** <<id_2.2.2,Memb. 2.>> Rectification of retention and evacuation, as costiveness, venery, bleeding at nose, months stopped, baths, &amp;c.
** <<id_2.2.3,Memb. 3.>> Air rectified, with a digression of the air
*** Naturally in the choice and site of our country, dwelling-place, to be hot and moist, light, wholesome, pleasant &amp;c.
*** Artificially, by often change of air, avoiding winds, fogs, tempests, opening windows, perfumes, &amp;c.
** <<id_2.2.4,Memb. 4.>> Exercise
*** Of body and mind, but moderate, as hawking, hunting, riding, shooting, bowling, fishing, fowling, walking in fair fields, galleries, tennis, bar.
*** Of mind, as chess, cards, tables &amp;c., to see plays, masks, &amp;c., serious studies, business, all honest recreations.
** <<id_2.2.5,Memb. 5.>> Rectification of waking and terrible dreams, &amp;c.
** <<id_2.2.6,Memb. 6.>> Rectification of passions and perturbations of the mind. ♎︎

[.braces]
* <<id_2.2.6,Memb. 6.>> Passions and perturbations of the mind rectified.
** From himself
*** <<id_2.2.6.1,Subsect. 1.>> By using all good means of help, confessing to a friend, &amp;c.
*** Avoiding all occasions of his infirmity.
*** Not giving way to passions, but resisting to his utmost.
** or from his friends.
*** <<id_2.2.6.2,Subsect. 2.>> By fair and foul means, counsel, comfort, good persuasion, witty devices, fictions, and, if it be possible, to satisfy his mind.
*** <<id_2.2.6.3,Subsect. 3.>> Music of all sorts aptly applied.
*** <<id_2.2.6.4,Subsect. 4.>> Mirth and merry company.
*** <<id_2.3.1,Sect. 3.>> A consolatory digression, containing remedies to all discontents and passions of the mind.
**** <<id_2.3.1,Memb. 1.>> General discontents and grievances satisfied.
**** <<id_2.3.2,Memb. 2.>> Particular discontents, as deformity of body, sickness, baseness of birth, &amp;c.
**** <<id_2.3.3,Memb. 3.>> Poverty and want, such calamites and adversities.
**** <<id_2.3.4,Memb. 4.>> Against servitude, loss of liberty, imprisonment, banishment, &amp;c.
**** <<id_2.3.5,Memb. 5.>> Against vain fears, sorrows for death of friends, or otherwise.
**** <<id_2.3.6,Memb. 6.>> Against envy, livor, hatred, malice, emulation, ambition, and self-love, &amp;c.
**** <<id_2.3.7,Memb. 7.>> Against repulses, abuses, injuries, contempts, disgraces, contumelies, slanders, and scoffs, &amp;c.
**** <<id_2.3.8,Memb. 8.>> Against all other grievous and ordinary symptoms of this disease of melancholy.

* [[gemini.2]]♊︎ Chirurgical physic, which consists of <<id_2.4.3,Memb. 3.>>
** Phlebotomy, to all parts almost, and all the distinct species.
** With knife, horseleeches.
** Cupping-glasses.
** Cauteries, and searing with hot irons, boring.
** Dropax and sinapismus.
** Issues to several parts, and upon several occasions.

[.braces]
* [[cancer.2]]♋︎ <<id_2.5.1,Sect. 5.>> Cure of head-melancholy. <<id_2.5.1,Memb. 1.>>
** <<id_2.5.1.1,1. Subsect.>> Moderate diet, meat of good juice, moistening, easy of digestion.
** Good air.
** Sleep more than ordinary.
** Excrements daily to be voided by art or nature.
** Exercise of body and mind not too violent, or too remiss, passions of the mind, and perturbations to be avoided.
** <<id_2.5.1.2,Subsect. 2.>> Bloodletting, if there be need, or that the blood be corrupt, in the arm, forehead, &amp;c., or with cupping-glasses.
** <<id_2.5.1.3,Subsect. 3.>> Preparatives and purgers.
*** Preparatives; as syrup of borage, bugloss, epithyme, hops, with their distilled waters, &amp;c.
*** Purgers; as Montanus, and Matthiolus __helleborismus, Quercetanus__, syrup of hellebore, extract of hellebore, pulvis Hali, antimony prepared, __Rulandi aqua mirabilis__; which are used, if gentler medicines will not take place, with Arnoldus, __vinum buglossatum__, senna, cassia, mirobalanes, __aurum potabile__, or before Hamech, Pil. Indae, Hiera, Pil. de lap. Armeno, lazuli.
** <<id_2.5.1.4,Subsect. 4.>> Averters.
*** Cardan's nettles, frictions, clysters, suppositories, sneezings, masticatories, nasals, cupping-glasses.
*** To open the haemorrhoids with horseleeches, to apply horseleeches to the forehead without scarification, to the shoulders, thighs.
*** Issues, boring, cauteries, hot irons in the suture of the crown.
** <<id_2.5.1.5,Subsect. 5.>> Cordials, resolvers, hinderers.
*** A cup of wine or strong drink.
*** Bezars stone, amber, spice.
*** Conserves of borage, bugloss, roses, fumitory.
*** Confection of Alchermes.
*** __Electuarium laetificans Galeni et Rhasis__, &amp;c.
*** __Diamargaritum frig. diaboraginatum__, &amp;c.

*** Irrigations of the head, with the decoctions of nymphea, lettuce, mallows, &amp;c.
*** Epithymes, ointments, bags to the heart.
*** Fomentations of oil for the belly.
*** Baths of sweet water, in which were sod mallows, violets, roses, water-lilies, borage flowers, ramsheads, &amp;c.
*** To procure sleep, and are

***** Simples

* [[leo.2]]♌︎ <<id_2.5.2,2. Memb.>> Cure of melancholy over the body.
** Diet, preparatives, purges, averters, cordials, correctors, as before.
** Phlebotomy in this kind more necessary, and more frequent.
** To correct and cleanse the blood with fumitory, senna, succory, dandelion, endive, &amp;c.

[.braces]
* [[virgo.2]]♍︎ Cure of hypochondriacal or windy melancholy. <<id_2.5.3,3. Memb.>>
** <<id_2.5.3.1,Subsect. 1>> Phlebotomy, if need require.
** Diet, preparatives, averters, cordials, purgers, as before, saving that they must not be so vehement.
** Use of pennyroyal, wormwood, centaury sod, which alone hath cured many.
** To provoke urine with aniseed, daucus, asarum, &amp;c., and stools, if need be, by clysters and suppositories.
** To respect the spleen, stomach, liver, hypochondries.
** To use treacle now and then in winter.
** To vomit after meals sometimes, if it be inveterate.
** <<id_2.5.3.2,Subsect. 2.>> To expel wind.
*** Inwardly Taken,
**** Simples,
***** Roots,
- Galanga, gentian, enula, angelica, calamus aromaticus, zedoary, china, condite ginger, &amp;c.
***** Herbs,
- Pennyroyal, rue, calamint, bay leaves, and berries, scordium, bethany, lavender, camomile, centaury, wormwood, cumin, broom, orange pills.
***** Spices,
- Saffron, cinnamon, mace, nutmeg, pepper, musk, zedoary with wine, &amp;c.
***** Seeds,
- Aniseed, fennel-seed, ammi, cary, cumin, nettle, bays, parsley, grana paradisi.
**** or Compounds, as
***** Dianisum, diagalanga, diaciminum, diacalaminthes, electuarium de baccis lauri, benedicta laxativa, &amp;c. pulvia carminativus, and pulvis descrip. Antidotario Florentine, aromaticum, rosatum, Mithridate.
*** or Outwardly used, as cupping-glasses to the hypochonries without scarification, oil of camomile, rue, aniseed, their decoctions, &amp;c.
