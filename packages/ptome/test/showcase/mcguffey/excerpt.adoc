[.lesson-title]
A KIND BROTHER.

. A boy was once sent from home
to take a basket of things to his grand{shy}mother.
. The basket was so full that it was
very heavy. So his little brother went
with him, to help carry the load.
. They put a pole under the handle
of the basket, and each then took
hold of an end of the pole. In this
way they could carry the basket very
nicely.
. Now the older boy thought, “My
brother Tom does not know about this
pole.

== Lesson III.

[.lesson-title]
WILLIE’S LETTER.

[.script.right]
New York, Dec. 10, 1878.

[.script.salute]
Dear Santa Claus:

[.script]
Papa is going to give
me a Christmas tree, and he
says that you will put nice
things on it if I ask you. I would
like a gun that will shoot, and
a rubber ball that I can throw
hard, and that will not break
Mamma’s windows or the big
glass in the parlor.

[.script.close]
Your little boy +
Willie.
