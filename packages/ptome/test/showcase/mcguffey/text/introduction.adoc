[.intro-text.first,margin-top=14.31pt]
[.term]#Punctuation Marks# are used to make
the sense more clear.

[.intro-text,margin-top=3.43pt]
A [.term]#Period# (.) is used at the end of a sen{shy}tence,
and after an abbreviation; as,

[.example,margin-top=0.29pt]
James was quite sick. Dr. Jones was called to see him.

<<<

// page 10
[.intro-text,margin-top=-0.01pt]
An [.term]#Interrogation Mark# (?) is used at
the end of a question; as,

[.example,margin-top=-0.71pt]
Where is John going?

[.intro-text,margin-top=7.16pt]
An [.term]#Exclamation Mark# (!) is used after
words or sentences expressing some strong
feeling; as,

[.example,margin-top=-0.11pt]
Alas, my noble boy! that thou shouldst die!

[.intro-text,margin-top=6.96pt]
The [.term]#Comma# (,), [.term]#Semicolon# (;), and
[.term]#Colon# (:) are used to separate the parts
of a sentence.

[.intro-text,margin-top=5.87pt]
The [.term]#Hyphen# (-) is used to join the parts
of a compound word; as, __text-book__: it is also
used at the end of a line in print or script,
when a word is divided; as in the word
“__sentence__,” near the bottom of page 9.
