=== ¶ Easter-wings.

[verse.sideways.clockwise.in-flow.wings,margin-top=56.5pt]
____
My tender age in sorrow did beginne:
[.i100]##{nbsp}##And still with sicknesses and shame
[.i195]##{nbsp}##Thou didst so punish sinne,
[.i285]##{nbsp}##That I became
[.i385]##{nbsp}##Most thinne.
[.i400]##{nbsp}##With thee
[.i285]##{nbsp}##Let me combine,
[.i190]##{nbsp}##And feel this day thy victorie:
[.i100]##{nbsp}##For, if I imp my wing on thine,
Affliction shall advance the flight in me.
