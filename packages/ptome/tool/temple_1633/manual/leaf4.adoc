[.prose.goes-on,ocr=0,first="having been uneffectually attempted by publick col-"]
having been uneffectually attempted by publick col-
lections, was in the end by his own and some few
others private free-will-offerings succesfully effected.
With the remembrance whereof, as of an especiall
good work, when a friend went about to comfort him
on his death-bed, he made answer, __It is a good work,__
__if it be sprinkled with the bloud of Christ:__ otherwise
then in this respect he could finde nothing to glorie or
comfort himself with, neither in this, nor in any other
thing.

[.prose.indent,ocr=11,first="And these are but a few of many that might be said,"]
And these are but a few of many that might be said,
which we have chosen to premise as a glance to some
parts of the ensuing book, and for an example to the
Reader. We conclude all with his own Motto, with
which he used to conclude all things that might seem
to tend any way to his own honour;

[.motto,ocr=18,first="Lesse then the least of Gods mercies."]
__Lesse then the least of Gods mercies.__
