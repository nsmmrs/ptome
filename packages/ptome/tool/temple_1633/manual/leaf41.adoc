=== ¶ Easter-wings.

[verse.sideways.clockwise.in-flow.wings,margin-top=52.5pt]
____
Lord, who createdst man in wealth and store,
[.i105]##{nbsp}##Though foolishly he lost the same,
[.i195]##{nbsp}##Decaying more and more,
[.i285]##{nbsp}##Till he became
[.i360]##{nbsp}##Most poore:
[.i370]##{nbsp}##With thee
[.i280]##{nbsp}##O let me rise
[.i195]##{nbsp}##As larks, harmoniously,
[.i95]##{nbsp}##And sing this day thy victories:
Then shall the fall further the flight in me.
____
