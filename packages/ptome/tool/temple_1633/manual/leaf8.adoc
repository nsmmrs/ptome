[.v,ocr=10,first="Beware of lust: it doth pollute and foul"]
Beware of lust: it doth pollute and foul
Whom God in Baptisme washt with his own blood.
It blots thy lesson written in thy soul;
The holy lines cannot be understood.
[.i105]##{nbsp}##How dare those eyes upon a Bible look,
[.i105]##{nbsp}##Much lesse towards God, whose lust is all their book?

[.v,ocr=17,first="Wholly abstain, or wed. Thy bounteous Lord"]
Wholly abstain, or wed. Thy bounteous Lord
Allows thee choise of paths: take no by-wayes;
But gladly welcome what he doth afford;
Not grudging, that thy lust hath bounds and staies.
[.i105]##{nbsp}##Continence hath his joy: weigh both; and so
[.i105]##{nbsp}##If rottennesse have more, let Heaven go.

[.v,ocr=23,first="If God had laid all common, certainly"]
If God had laid all common, certainly
Man would have been th'incloser: but since now
God hath impal'd us, on the contrary
Man breaks the fence, and every ground will plough.
[.i105]##{nbsp}##O what were man, might he himself misplace!
[.i105]##{nbsp}##Sure to be crosse he would shift feet and face.
