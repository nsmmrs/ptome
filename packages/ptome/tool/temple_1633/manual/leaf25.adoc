[#the-altar,base=1297,first="The Altar."]
=== The Altar.

[.altar.altar-1,base=1605,first="A broken ALTAR, Lord, thy servant reares;"]
A broken [.spaced-cap]##A##[.spaced]##LTAR##, Lord, thy servant reares;
Made of a heart, and cemented with teares:

[.altar.altar-2,base=2015,first="Whose parts are as thy hand did frame;"]
Whose parts are as thy hand did frame;
No workmans tool hath touch'd the same.

[.altar.altar-3,base=2433,first="A HEART alone"]
A [.spaced-cap]##H##[.spaced]##EART## alone
Is such a stone,
As nothing but
Thy power doth cut.
Wherefore each part
Of my hard heart
Meets in this frame,
To praise thy name:

[.altar.altar-4,base=4079,first="That if I chance to hold my peace,"]
That if I chance to hold my peace,
These stones to praise thee may not cease.

[.altar.altar-5,base=4495,first="O let thy blessed SACRIFICE be mine,"]
O let thy blessed [.spaced-cap]##S##[.spaced]##ACRIFICE## be mine,
And sanctifie this [.spaced-cap]##A##[.spaced]##LTAR## to be thine.
