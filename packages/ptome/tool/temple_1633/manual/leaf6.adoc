== ¶ The Dedication.

[.v.dropcap.ded,ocr=9,first="Ord, my first fruits present themselves to thee;"]
LOrd, my first fruits present themselves to thee;
Yet not mine neither: for from thee they came,
And must return. Accept of them and me,
And make us strive, who shall sing best thy Name.
[.i95]##{nbsp}##Turn their eyes hither, who shall make a gain:
[.i95]##{nbsp}##Theirs, who shall hurt themselves or me, refrain.
